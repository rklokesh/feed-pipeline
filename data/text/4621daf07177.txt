PUBLIC NOTICE
Dated: 05.11.2026

Subject: Extension of Last Date for Submission of Online Application Form for
Joint CSIR UGC-NET December 2026.

The National Testing Agency (NTA) is conducting the Joint CSIR-UGC NET Examination December-
2026, for determine the eligibility of Indian nationals for 'award of Junior Research Fellowship and
appointment as Assistant Professor’, 'appointment as Assistant Professor and admission to Ph.D.' and
‘admission to Ph.D. only' in Indian universities and colleges through Computer Based Test (CBT) mode.

In interest of candidates, it has been decided to extend the last date for submission of the Online Application
Form for Joint CSIR UGC-NET December 2026 Examination up to 090 November 2026 (11:50 PM)
without any late fee to facilitate candidates who have not yet completed their application process. The
revised schedule is as follows:

Activity Revised Date
Last date for submission of Online Application Form 090 November 2026 (up to 11:50 PM)
Last date for successful transaction of examination fee 09% November 2026 (up to 11:50 PM)

Candidates are advised to complete their application process well before the revised deadline to avoid any last-
minute technical difficulties.

All other terms and conditions mentioned in the Information Bulletin and Public Notice shall remain
unchanged.

Candidates are advised to regularly visit the official NTA websites https://nta.ac.in/ and
https://csirnet.nta.nic.in/ for the latest updates.
