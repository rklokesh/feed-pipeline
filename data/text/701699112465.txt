RAILWAY RECRUITMENT CELL
NORTHERN RAILWAY
NOTICE
GDCE Recruitment against Notification No. 01/2023/GDCE - Re-examination
of Candidates due to Mismatch in Trade/Examination Group
It is hereby informed that certain candidates who appeared for the CBT for the post of
ALP/Technician under the GDCE Recruitment against Notification No. 01/2023/GDCE
held on 27.07.2026, 31.07.2026 &% 07.08.2026 have represented regarding a mismatch
between the Trade/ Examination Group actually opted by them in their online application
and the Trade/Examination Group reflected during the CBT.
The matter has been examined with reference to the representations received from the
candidates and, on verification, certain candidates have been identified as having such
mismatch.
Accordingly, the identified candidates will be given an opportunity toi ndicate whether they
are willing to appear for re-examination in the concerned Trade /Examination Group
corresponding to their original online application. The information regarding the identified
candidates will be communicated to them through SMS and email at the mobile numbers
and email addresses provided by them at the time of submission of their application
forms.
The candidates concerned are required to submit their consent/willingness for re
examination within the stipulated period through the online mode. A link is being provided
for the same. The last date for submission of the consent is 12.11.2026. Candidates who
do not submit their consent within the prescribed time shall be treated as not willing to
appear for re-examination, and their mnarks obtained in the examination already appeared
for shall be considered for further processing, subject to fulfilment of other applicable
conditions.
Candidates who submit their consent and appear for the re-examination shall note that
their previous examination shall stand cancelled and shall not be considered for any
purpose. The marks obtained in the re-examination alone shall be taken into
consideration for further processing.
Further, if a can didate submits consent for re-examination but remains absent from the
re-examination, the marks obtained in the previous examination shall also not be
considered, and no marks shall be counted for such candidate for the concerned
examination.
The candidates are, therefore, advised to exercise their option carefully after
understanding the above consequences. Further action regarding re-examination shall be
taken strictly on the basis of the consent received from the concerned candidates.
Candidates are advised to regularly visit the official RRC/ Northern Railway website for
further updates and instructions.
