Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD)
in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026– Information regarding status of categories of
shortlisted candidates.
Staff Selection Commission has declared Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces
(CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026 on 12.10.2026
2. The Commission has received representations from some candidates seeking
clarification regarding the categories of candidates reflected in List-I (Female) and List-II
(Male) of the candidates provisionally shortlisted for the next stage of the examination.
3. In this regard, it is clarified that certain candidates, who had applied under the
SC/ST/OBC/EWS/ESM categories at time of filling up of their Online Application Form, have
qualified the Computer Based Examination (CBE) on the basis of the standards prescribed for
Unreserved (UR) candidates in terms of provisions of the Examination Notice. Accordingly,
their category was reflected as UR in the lists (List-I and List-II) published earlier on the
website of the Commission.
4. To ensure clarity, the Commission has now uploaded the detailed lists on its website,
after including both the categories: the actual category under which the candidates applied and
the category against which they have been considered, as detailed below:
List-I: Female candidates provisionally shortlisted for appearing in PET/PST
List-II: Male candidates provisionally shortlisted for appearing in PET/PST other than
SSF.
5. All other terms and conditions of the result of the Computer Based Examination (CBE) for
the Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD)
in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026, shall remain unchanged.
