CEN NO.RRC-08/2024: NOTICE NO.7 DATED 20.10.2026
REGARDING DOCUMENT VERIFICATION TO RECRUITMENT FOR
VARIOUS POSTS OF LEVEL-1.

1. In continuation to Notice no-06, dated 13.10.2026, the candidates
shortlisted for Document verification and medical examination are advised
to be ready for the same, which is scheduled to commence from 02.11.2026
onwards at the respective venues. No request for change of venue or DV
date shall be entertained.

2. The list of 4749 candidates (GEN-3343, CCAA - 953, ESM-289 and PwBD-
164) shortlisted for Document Verification (DV) and Medical Examination
(ME), along with the venue and scheduled dates of DV, is enclosed as
Annexure-I. There is a shortfall of 33 PwBD candidates. Further, two posts
(one OBC and one SC) have been kept reserved for two CCAA candidates in
compliance with the directions of the Hon’ble CAT/Lucknow in OA No.
387/2025. One PwBD-LD post has also been kept reserved in compliance
with the directions of the Hon’ble CAT, Principal Bench, New Delhi.

3. A link to download call letter for Document Verification will be made
available on the website shortly. E-Mail /SMS to this effect will be sent on

the registered mail ID/ Mobile Number of the candidates advising them to
download their E Call Letter.

4. The block date for supplementary Document Verification (DV) has been
fixed as 03.12.2026 for candidates who remain absent on their scheduled
date of DV. they are directed to report for the document verification at
the same venue as mentioned in their e- call letter. They should report
with E-Call letter for DV already issued. No separate E-Call letter for the

block date will be issued. No further request for DV on any other date
shall be entertained.

3. The result is purely provisional and shall remain subject to the outcome of the
final judgment of the Hon’ble High Court Telangana in WP no 7985 /2025 and
other pending Court cases.

Please note that in other matters, the terms and conditions laid down in the

If any candidate is found obstructing the conduct of the DV or creating
disturbances at the DV venue, his/her candidature shall be summarily
cancelled. Such candidate shall also be liable to be debarred from future
Test of the Railway and legal/criminal proceeding could be initiated against
him/her. It may be noted that no re-DV would be conducted if it is found
that the DV was disrupted on account of instigation by the candidates.

In case of walkout, for any reason whatsoever no re-DV will be taken.

There shall be no claim for alternate appointment on the ground of medical
unfitness, if any. The candidates fit in lower medical category i.e Aye-three

and below can make appeal to the competent Authority as per guidelines
already available on RRC website. '

All necessary care has been taken while preparing the list for eligible
candidates for documents verification but inadvertent human errors cannot

be over ruled. RRC/Northern Railway reserves the right to correct such
mistakes whenever it is noticed.

Candidates shall make their own arrangement of stay for 3-4 days, if
required, at the venue. No TA/DA is admissible for the same.

Railway shall not be responsible for any loss/damage caused to candidates
during the Documents Verification.

Candidates may please note that merely being called for Document
Verification does not confer any right on them for appointment to the notified
posts.

Beware of Touts and job racketeers trying to deceive by false promises of
securing job in Railways either through influence or by use of unfair and
unethical means. RRB/RRC never appoints any agent(s) or coaching
centre(s) for action on its behalf. Candidates are warned against any such
claims being made by persons/agencies. Candidates are selected purely as
per merit. Beware of unscrupulous elements and do not fall in their trap.
Candidates attempting to influence RRB/RRC directly or indirectly shall be
disqualified and legal action can be intimated against them.
