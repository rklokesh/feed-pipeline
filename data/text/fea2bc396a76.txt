NOTICE

CLOSURE OF THE ANNUAL TRANSFER PROCESS — CLOSURE OF THE ANNUAL TRANSFER PROCESS — 2026

It is to bring to the Notice of all concerned that Kendriya Vidyalaya
Sangathan has completed and closed annual transfer process-2026 on
dated 30.10.2026. Henceforth, no further representation / request for
transfer / review of transfer order or cancellation of transfer etc. shall be
entertained by KVS. Further, all representations submitted by the
employees for transfer / review / cancellation / modification of transfer
order etc. for which orders have not been issued, stand disposed of with
the closure of the transfer cycle, in adherence with Transfer Policy.

This issues with the approval of the competent authority.
