Important Instructions:

1.

Candidates can apply for Common Entrance Examination for ACC-January 2027 through
the “Online” mode only through the website https:/ /exams.nta.nic.in/acccee/

Submission of the Online Application Form may be done by accessing the NTA website
https:/ /exams.nta.nic.in/acccee/. The Application Form in any other mode will not be accepted.

3. Only one application is to be submitted by a candidate.

‘

a

Candidates must strictly follow the instructions given in the Information Bulletin
available on the NTA website. Candidates not complying with the instructions shall be
summarily disqualified.

Candidates must use the same Mobile Number as registered with the Army Education
Directorate while filling and submitting the Online Application Form, as all
information/communication will be sent by NTA through e-mail on the registered e-mail
address or SMS on the registered Mobile Number only.

In case candidate faces difficulty in applying for Common Entrance Examination for ACC-
January 2027, please contact on 011 - 40759000 / 011 - 69227700 or e-mail at acccee@nta.ac.in
for any clarification.

Candidates must fill in their particulars carefully, as no changes will be allowed after the
final submission of the Application Form. As far as possible, candidates are advised to use
Aadhaar verification while completing the Application Form, as this will facilitate the
registration and examination process.

Dates mentioned above are tentative, and applicants will be informed in case there is any
change due to any reason.
