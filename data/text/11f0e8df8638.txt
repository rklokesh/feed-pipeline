IMPORTANT NOTICE FOR PwBD CATEGORY CANDIDATES

This Notice is issued to the above Notification for CRP SPL —XVI dated 01.07.2026 for the
purpose of clarification.

Vide Clause B (III) of the said Notification it has been stated that “the post is identified
suitable for the Persons with undernoted categories of disabilities as defined in the
Schedule of RPWD Act 2016 and notified by the Department of Empowerment of Persons
with Disabilities (Divyangjan) from time to time”.

Disabilities (Divyangjan), Government of India, a list of posts suitable for persons with
