PUBLIC NOTICE
As notified vide Public Notice dated 08.05.2026, the online application process for the 22nd
Edition of CTET was initially open from 11.05.2026 to 10.06.2026. Subsequently, vide
Public Notice dated 25.08.2026, an additional opportunity was provided to all eligible
candidates who could not avail themselves of the earlier application window, and online
application portal was opened from 25.08.2026 to 01.09.2026.
The facility for online correction in particulars, as applicable, will be available on the
official CTET website https://ctet.nic.in from 07.09.2026 to 10.09.2026.
Candidates who had successfully submitted their applications during the earlier
application window from 11.05.2026 to 10.06.2026 may also make corrections in their
particulars, as applicable, during the above correction window.
No correction shall be allowed after 10.09.2026 under any circumstances.
