Important Notice
Subject: Rescheduling of PET/PST dates of SSC Constable (GD) Examination, 2026,
in case of clash with SI in DP and other CAPFs, 2026 (Paper-1) and SI in DP
and other CAPFs, 2025 (Paper-2) Examination – reg.
It has come to the notice of the Commission that for some candidates the dates of the
Physical Efficiency Test (PET)/Physical Standard Test (PST) of SSC Constable (GD)
Examination, 2026 clash with the dates of SI in DP and CAPFs Exam, 2026 (Paper-I) and SI in
DP and CAPFs Exam, 2025 (Paper-II) scheduled from 23.11.2026 to 28.11.2026.
2. In order to provide relief to the candidates facing such a clash, candidates appearing in
both examinations are advised to bring the matter to the notice of the PET/ PST conducting
authority through the email address given below, along with their Name, Roll
Number/Registration Number, details of the examinations and relevant supporting documents.
Email ID: dcrecruitmentcrpf@gmail.com
3. Such cases will be examined for rescheduling of the PET/PST dates of the SSC Constable
(GD) Examination, 2026, as appropriate. Candidates are advised to mention their Name,
Registration/Roll Number, and the dates of both examinations in their email for necessary action.
