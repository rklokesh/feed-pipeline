(Recruitment of various posts of Paramedical Category)
Notice regarding city intimation slip & helpdesk link for CBT
1. The link for viewing Exam City & date and downloading of Travel Authority for SC/ST
candidates for Computer Based Test (CBT) is live on the official websites of all RRBs.
Candidates can view their exam city 10 days (tentatively) prior the respective exam date
which is scheduled on 23.11.2026 and 25.11.2026.
2. Downloading of E-Call letters will start 4 days (tentatively) prior to exam dates mentioned in the
Exam City and Date intimation link.
3. Aadhaar linked biometric authentication of candidates will be done in the exam center prior to
entry in the exam hall. Candidates are required to bring their original Aadhaar Card or printout
of e-verified Aadhaar. Candidates are advised to authenticate their identification through
Aadhaar verification, if not done already, by logging in with their credentials at
www.rrbapply.gov.in to facilitate smooth entry into the examination center.
4. Candidates, including those who have verified their Aadhaar during the application submission
stage, are advised in their own interest to ensure that their Aadhaar remains in unlocked
condition in the UIDAI system before coming to exam centre to avoid inconvenience and
facilitate registration and exam-related processes on the exam day.
5. Aadhaar Card should be updated with correct name, date of birth (as per 10th Class certificate),
latest photograph, biometrics (if not updated upon reaching the age of 15 years), address and
father’s name.
6. In case of any queries or clarification needed, the Helpdesk contact No. 7353921119 can
be used by candidates. Helpdesk timing - Monday to Saturday - 09:00 am to 06:00 pm.
7. Candidates are advised to refer only to the official websites of RRBs for latest updates on the
recruitment process. Please do not get misled by unauthenticated sources.
8. Beware of the touts who may misguide candidates with the false promises of getting them
selected for the job on illegal consideration. The recruitment process in the RRB exams is fully
computerized and the selection is based purely on the merit of candidates.
