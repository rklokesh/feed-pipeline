Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
CEN. No. 06/2026 Recruitment for various posts of Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate))
(English)
01 What is the opening date of Application for CEN No. 06/2026?
The opening date of application is 08.10.2026.
02 What is the closing date for submission of Application for CEN No. 06/2026?
The closing date for submission of online application is 06.11.2026 @23.59Hrs. And the last date for payment of
the application fee for submitted applications is 08.11.2026.
03 What are the dates for modification of application?
Modification window will open only after closing date for submission of application and will be active from
09.11.2026 to 18.11.2026. If candidates wish to modify, change or correct any details except details filled in the
‘Create an Account’ form (including email id and mobile number) and chosen RRB, they may do so by paying a
modification fee of Rs.250/- (non-refundable) for each occasion. (Please Note: Details filled in ‘Create an Account’
form and ‘Chosen RRB’ cannot be modified).
04 My final results are expected after closing date for submission of application. Can I apply?
No, Candidates waiting for final results of prescribed educational/technical qualifications are not eligible to apply.
Candidates should possess prescribed educational/technical qualifications on or before the closing date for
submission of application for this CEN.
05 What are the requirements to ‘Create an Account’?
Candidates should have their own active mobile number and a valid personal email ID for communication during
the entire duration of recruitment, as communication with them will be through SMS and/or email.
06 I have created an account for previous CENs of railways. Should I create another account for CEN 06/2026?
NO, Candidates who have already created an account during any notification published by Railway Recruitment
Board in the year 2024/2025/2026 are not required to Create an Account again. They should login using the same
username and password to apply for CEN 06/2026.
07 What is the procedure to ‘Create an Account’?
If a candidate has not yet created an account on the RRB application portal, they must first create an account
before proceeding to fill up the application for this CEN. To do so, click on the "Apply" tab on the application link
home page and then select "Create an Account". Fill in the required details in the online account creation form and
create a password. The password should contain a combination of capital letters, small letters, numbers and
special characters. Upon successful “Creation of the Account”, a confirmation SMS and email will be sent to the
candidate.
08 Can I edit the ‘Create an Account’ details?
Candidates CANNOT edit any details of the ‘Create an account’ once the account is created.
09 What is next procedure after ‘Create an Account’?
 After Create an Account, Log-in to the application in rrbapply.gov.in portal using RRB credentials or
AADHAAR credentials.
 There are five broad sections to complete the online submission of application like Personal Details, Other
Details, Educational Qualification, Upload Profile Documents, Preview and Preference & Submit.
 The details filled in “Create an Account” stage will pre-populate in the application which cannot be edited.
Begin by entering all details, starting with Personal Details. After entering personal details, save and
proceed to the next section and continue in this manner until you reach the last section i.e., Preview and
Preference & Submit, where a candidate can finally submit the online application for payment.
 After completion of fee payment process, the applicant will receive a confirmation on successful payment
through email & SMS from RRB. After that, a candidate can take a printout of the submitted application
containing the registration number.

10 Can mobile be used for filling up of application?
Yes, smart mobile phone may be used for filling the application using web Browser.
11 What is the procedure to login?
Enter registered mobile number and email ID followed by password and captcha and submit.
12 How many Pay Levels are there in CEN 06/2026?
There are two pay level in CEN 06/2026 (Level-5 & Level-6).
13 How to apply?
Kindly refer to Para 14.0 of CEN for details on how to apply. Candidates must read all the information and
instructions given in the detailed CEN carefully before filling the ONLINE application to prevent mistakes.
14 What is the use of application Registration Number?
Candidates are advised to note and remember their Registration Number of submitted application till the
completion of recruitment process. Registration Number will be used for further stages of recruitment process and
correspondence with RRBs. Refer to Para 16.0 (b) (iv) of the detailed CEN 06/2026.
15 What is the crucial date for PwBD certificate?
PwBD candidates must possess their disability certificate on or before closing date of online application.
16 Can I apply to different RRBs for same pay levels?
No. Candidates are allowed to apply for one RRB only.
17 Can I apply to more than one RRB?
No, you should not. Applying to more than one RRB will result in the rejection of all applications and will lead to
debarment from all future examinations of RRB and RRC.
18 Can I apply multiple applications to same RRB?
No, you should not. Multiple applications to same RRB will result in the rejection of all applications and will lead to
debarment from all future examinations of RRB and RRC.
19 I have undergone LASIK surgery. Am I eligible to apply?
Candidate’s undergone LASIK surgery is eligible for posts that require Medical Standards B2 & C2. Please refer
Note (c) of Para 3.0 of CEN 06/2026.
20 Why DigiLocker/Aadhaar Verification is required?
Digi Locker/Aadhaar validation is required for smoother process at different stages of recruitment for the
candidates themselves. Further, to ensure genuineness of candidature and to curb impersonation during CBTs.
RRBs will not share Aadhaar details with any third party or use them for any purpose other than the objectives of
this CEN. If applicants verify their identity & other primary details with alternate Photo IDs, they will be subjected
to significantly stricter & more detailed scrutiny at every stage of recruitment process.
21 What should I do, if I don’t have Aadhaar?
Candidates are advised, in their own interest, to authenticate their identity using Digilocker or Aadhaar during the
"Create an Account" stage. If they missed this stage, they can still authenticate during the online application
details filling process by using the “Verify Aadhaar" facility and providing their Aadhaar details. This must be
completed before final submission of application for a particular CEN. This will facilitate a smoother process at

22 What document should I produce if I have already changed my name?
Applicants must possess a Gazette Notification or any other legal document as applicable for name changes and
should be submitted at the time of CBTs & DV. Date of such change or application for change should be prior to
the date of submission of online application. Applicants should also have to fill the original name and the changed
name during ‘Create an Account’. In such cases, both the names will be reflected in the application.
23 What should I do if I forgot password to login My Account?
Click on the “Forgot Password” option in the “Sign into Your Account” section of the login page. Enter either
Aadhaar number or registered email ID/mobile number and date of birth. The revised password will be sent to the
candidate’s registered email ID only. Candidates have to login with the revised password sent to their registered
email ID.
24 Can I change my password?
Yes. Candidates should first login by using the old password. After logging in, Candidates can change their old
password by accessing the 'Change Password' option available on the upper right- hand side of the online
application page.
25 Can I change my ‘Chosen RRB’?
No, you cannot change the ‘chosen RRB’ after submission of application.
26 Can I edit my details during the application process?
Yes. Any details in the application other than the details entered during ‘Create an Account’ can be edited till the
application is submitted and registration number generated.
27 I have wrongly mentioned my community/caste in the application. Can I modify my community/caste after
submission of application?
Yes, editing of details is allowed during Modification window period only by paying prescribed fee. However, after
Modification window period is closed, editing of details is not allowed. You must submit Community and caste as
per the online application during the Document Verification process.
28 Can I edit my application details after submission of online application before making payment?
 No, candidates cannot edit details after submission of application.
 Candidates are advised to re-check the detailed filled by them in application preview at Preference and
Preview & Submit page for correctness and further submit application if all the information is correct since
no change is permitted after submission of application for payment. If any information is found incorrect at
application preview, they can click on ‘Edit’ button and rectify the incorrect information at respective page
of application module, then again preview application for correctness and submit the application.
 After submission of the application, pay the requisite examination fee and complete the payment process.
If a candidate wishes to modify, change or correct any details except details filled in the ‘Create an Account’
form details and chosen RRB, they may do so by paying a modification fee of Rs.250/- (non-refundable) for
each occasion during the modification window.
29 Who are eligible for ‘Free Travel Authority’ (free Railway Pass)?
Free Sleeper Class Railway Pass admissible to SC & ST candidates (if opted in application) will be provided with
their City Intimation Slip/e-call letter for various stages of selection viz., CBT/DV/Medical Examination (as
applicable) as per the details and valid caste certificate furnished in their ONLINE application. At the Community
section of the Personal Details page, they can avail the same by choosing the ‘Yes’ option from the drop-down
under ‘Do you wish to avail free rail travel pass for the exam’ and fill in ‘Nearest Railway Station’. Only candidates
who wish to avail the free rail travel pass will be asked to upload their Caste Certificate. Please refer to Para 19.0
(e) of the detailed CEN for complete information

30 How do I avail ‘Free Travel Authority’ (free Railway Pass)?
While booking train reservation and travelling, the original SC & ST caste certificate must be shown to the
authorized Railway staff concerned, for verification of the genuineness of the candidate. They will be allowed to
book train reservation by submitting a self-attested photocopy of City Intimation Slip/e-call letter and SC/ST
certificate at the ticket booking counter. SC/ST candidates should carry their original community certificate and an
original proof of identity for under taking journey, failing which they will be treated as travelling without ticket and
will be charged accordingly. Any misuse of this free travel facility shall lead to rejection of candidature at any stage
of the selection process, including debarment from future examinations. Please refer to Para 19.0 (e) of the
detailed CEN for complete information.
31 How can I receive the free travel facility?
In case of eligible SC & ST candidates, their City Intimation Slip/e-call letter itself will contain the free travel
authority (sleeper class railway pass). They will be allowed to book train reservation by submitting a self-attested
photocopy of their City Intimation Slip/e- call letter and SC/ST certificate at the ticket booking counter. For further
details, refer Para 19.0 (e) of CEN No. 06/2026
32 I am unable to upload my Caste Certificate.
Check whether the scanned document is in ‘PDF’ format and the size is less than 400 KB.
33 What is EBC?
EBC is ‘Economically Backward Class’. Economically Backward Class (EBC) candidates are those whose annual
family income is less than Rs.50,000/-. Candidates should not confuse Economically Backward Class with other
Backward Class (OBC) or Economically Weaker Section (EWS) or Extremely Backward Class. Refer para 7.3
34 What are the benefits given to EBC candidates?
EBC candidates are given ‘Fee concession’ only but they are not eligible for reservation benefits.
35 What will happen if a candidate failed to produce EBC Certificate?
If a candidate availed fee concession as EBC, he/she should have a valid Income Certificate (as per Annexure III A of
the CEN) or BPL Card or any other certificate issued by Central Government under the recognized poverty
alleviation program or IZZAT MST issued by Railways before submission of online application and will require to
submit at the time of Document Verification, failing which his/her candidature will be rejected. Refer Para 7.3 of
detailed CEN for complete information.
36 Are there reserved posts for EBC?
No. EBC candidates are eligible only for fee concession. There is no reservation of posts for EBC candidates.
37 Who is included under minorities?
Minorities include Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains and Zoroastrians (Parsis) which were given minority
status by the State/UT where the candidate belongs, subject to evasion/deletion/inclusion if any received till last
date of submission of online application (please refer Para 7.2 of CEN 06/2026).
38 What are the benefits given to Minorities candidates? What will happen if a candidate failed to produce an
affidavit for minority community declaration during document verification?
Minorities candidates are given ‘Fee concession’ only and not eligible for reservation benefits. Minority candidates
claiming waiver of examination fee will be required to submit an affidavit for minority community declaration (as
per Annexure IV of the CEN) on non-judicial stamp paper mentioning their minority community at the time of
Document Verification, failing which their candidature will be rejected.
39 What are the criteria for availing reservation under EWS?
Refer Para 8.13 of the detailed CEN No. 06/2026 available in the official websites of RRBs.
40 Who are all eligible for Fee Concession?
SC, ST, Ex-Servicemen, PwBD, female, transgender, Minorities and Economically Backward Class candidates are
eligible for fee relaxation. Please refer para 7.0 of CEN No.06/2026

41 What is the crucial date for reckoning age for this CEN-06/2026?
Crucial date for reckoning age for this CEN-06/2026 is 01-01-2027.
42 Crucial date for educational qualification? Can candidates whose result expected after closing date for
submission of online application apply?
Candidates (Existing/New) must possess their educational/ technical qualification on or before closing date for
submission of online application. Candidates waiting for final results of prescribed educational/technical
qualification are not eligible to apply.
43 What is the crucial date to claim SC/ST/OBC/EWS status?
Community /category status for SC, ST, OBC-NCL and EWS as on the closing date of submission of applications
against this CEN shall only be considered for reservation and related benefits as per eligibility. Any change in this
status there after shall not be considered. Please refer para 8.0 (Vertical Reservation) of the detailed CEN for
complete information. Candidates seeking reservation on the basis of SC/ ST/ OBC/ EWS status must ensure that
they possess the caste/ community certificate as on the closing date for submission of application of this CEN.
During DV, such candidates should produce their original caste/community certificate (in prescribed format) valid
as on the closing date for submission of application.
44 I have done degree from recognized University or its equivalent; can I apply for the posts of NTPC (Graduate)
Category?
The Education Qualification have been given in “ANNEXURE-A” of CEN-06/2026 in POST PARAMETERS.
45 I am currently serving in the Armed Forces. Can I apply as an Ex- Serviceman?
Please refer para 10.2 of CEN. For the armed services personnel who have been serving in armed forces on the
closing date of application submission, shall submit “Employment certificate with probable date of discharge from
service” as per Annexure VI (A) which is VALID as on closing date of submission of Online application for this CEN.
Further, a self-declaration of Undertaking as per Annexure VI is to be submitted to claim Ex-servicemen
reservation benefits, by all at the time of Document Verification. Please refer to para 10.0 of the detailed CEN for
complete information.
46 I have already secured civil employment as an Ex-Serviceman. Am I eligible for Ex-Serviceman benefits against
this CEN?
Ex-Serviceman candidates who have already secured civil employment under Central Government in Group 'C' &
erstwhile Group 'D' (including PSUs, Autonomous Bodies/Statutory Bodies, Nationalized Banks etc.) after availing
of the benefits given to Ex- Servicemen will be permitted only the benefit of age relaxation as prescribed for Ex-
Servicemen for securing another civil employment in a higher grade or cadre in Group 'C'/erstwhile Group 'D'
under Central Government. Such candidates will not be considered against the vacancies reserved for Ex-
Servicemen. Refer Para 10.0 of the detailed CEN No. 06/2026.
47 Can I submit common application for other post?
Only one online application through unified official RRB website (https://rrb.indianrailways.gov.in) is required to be
submitted by the candidate. The candidate must provide order of post/zone preference for any or all of the notified
posts for which the candidate is eligible and interested. Candidates with partial options will be considered only for
the specific Railway Zones and posts/categories opted by them.
48 Who should produce NOC at the time of Document Verification
Candidates serving (including those undergoing induction training/probation) in any Central/ State Government
Department including Railways or Public Sector Undertakings, may apply directly to the RRBs duly informing their
employer. Shortlisted candidates should produce an NOC from the employer on the date of DV, failing which their
candidature will be cancelled. It is the sole responsibility of the candidate to submit the NOC from his/her current
employer within the prescribed time limit during/at the time of Document verification/ empanelment/
appointment, failing which his/ her candidature/ empanelment/ appointment will be rejected & cancelled. Please
refer to Para No. 12.0 of the CEN.
