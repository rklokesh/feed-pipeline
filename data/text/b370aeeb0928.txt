Subject: Sanction of 45 posts of Section Officer in Pay Level-? in lieu of surrendering
57 posts of Assistant Section Officer in Pay Level-6 in Kendriya Vidyalaya
Sangathan - regarding.
The Ministry of Education, Department of School Education & Literacy,
Government of India vide its letter No. 3-30/2011-UT-2 dated 09.11.2026, has conveyed
the approval of the Department of Expenditure, Ministry of Finance, Government of India
for creation of 45 posts of Section Officer in Pay Level-7 in Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan
(KVS) against surrender of 57 posts of Superintendent (now designated as Assistant
Section Officer) in Pay Level-6.
2. The aforesaid approval has been accorded by the Department of Expenditure vide
its ID Note No. 11(15)/Education/12/E.Coord.I dated 04.11.2026, with the approval of the
Secretary (Exp en di ture).
3. A copy of the Ministry of Education, Department of School Education & Literacy,
Government of India communication dated 09.11.2026, along with the enclosed ID Note
of the Department of Expenditure, is circulated to all Deputy Commissioners, KVS
Regional Offices, for information.
4. The Regional Offices are requested to take note of the above approval. Further
instructions regarding implementation of the sanction, including cadre-related matters,
shall be issued separately by KVS Headquarters, as required.
