3.0 Final Selection of the candidates under different categories shall be decided and
results will be declared inter alia based on the guidelines issued by the Department of
Personnel & Training vide O.M. No. 36012/16/2019-Estt (Res.) dated 12.04.2022
subject to the final outcome of the following court cases:-

Hon’ble Supreme Court of India and connected matters (Jarnail Singh batch
of cases);
II. O.A. No. 3801 of 2025 in the matter of Mahaveer Prasad Gupta & Ors. Vs
Union of India & Anr.;
III. O.A. No. 3828 of 2025 in the matter of Som Dutt & Ors. Vs Union of India &
Ors.;
IV. O.A. No. 3863 of 2025 in the matter of Hemant Kumar & Ors. Vs Union of
India & Ors.; and
V. Any other cases in the connected matter.
4.0 The candidates may obtain information/clarification regarding their issue(s), if
any, in relation to the Examination under reference from the Commission’s Call
Centre (Tel. 011-40303444/24041001) from 09:00 AM to 08:00 PM. The result will
also be available on the UPSC web site i.e. www.upsc.gov.in. The marks, cut-offs, etc.
shall be made available on the website within fifteen days from the date of declaration
of final results of the Combined Stenographers’ (Grade ‘B’/Grade-‘I’) Limited
