CORRIGENDUM

Subject: Corrigendum to Office Memorandum F. No. NTA/OM/D/88/2026-ED-II dated 17.09.2026
regarding the procedure for issuance of Duplicate Scorecard.

With reference to the Office Memorandum cited above (hereinafter "the OM"), the following
corrections are hereby notified with immediate effect, with the approval of the Competent Authority:

1.

Paragraph 5.1(c): The following shall be added at the end: "Submission of such a report shall
not be mandatory, and it shall be furnished only if a report has been lodged by the Applicant."

Paragraph 5.2: Paragraph 5.2 shall be deleted
Paragraph 5.3: Paragraph 5.3 shall be deleted.

Paragraph 7.2: The fee shall be paid, through the online payment gateway integrated with
the NTA portal referred to in paragraph 6.1 by way of NEFT transaction using the following
particulars:

Bank name: Canara Bank

Account holder name: National Testing Agency

Account No. : 86882010019080

IFSC code: CNRB0000268

Where online payment is not feasible, the fee may remitted by Demand Draft drawn in favour
of "National Testing Agency" payable at New Delhi.

Paragraph 7.3: Paragraph 7.3 shall be deleted. No Physical copy of the duplicate score card
can be provided as, it is an electronically generated document.

Annexures I, II and III: The application form, affidavit and indemnity-cum-undertaking shall
be read in conformity with the above corrections. Portions relating to loss, theft, mutilation,
damage or destruction of the scorecard, lodging of a police/ embassy report, efforts to trace
the score card, and surrender of the original shall apply only where relevant, and the
Applicant may strike out the portions not applicable and state the actual reason. In Recital B
of Annexure III, the reference to the Office Memorandum shall be read as "F. No.
NTA/OM/D/88/2026-ED-II dated 17.09.2026".

All other contents of the OM remain unchanged.
