ADDENDUM NOTICE
Employment Notification No. RRC/WR/01/2026(Sports Quota) Date of Issue: 27/08/2026
Candidates who have applied for Weightlifting (Men & Women) against the above Notification may
please note that:
1) In terms of Railway Board’s letter No 2025/E (Sports)/4(3)/12/WLG (Wt. Change) (E.No.3505702)
RBE No. 89/2026 dated 28-10-2026, recruitment under Sports Quota in the Weightlifting (Men &
Women) discipline for the year 2026-27 onwards shall be conducted as per the revised weight
categories. These norms will apply to recruitment through Talent Scouting as well as Open
Advertisement and also to the cases where trials are yet to be conducted.
2) The sports achievement certificates issued for the events concluded prior to 01/08/2026 for revised
weight categories shall continue to be considered eligible for recruitment and trial purposes, provided
such certificates are valid as per extant instructions.
3) As per para-2(iii) of RBE 89/2026 dated 28-10-2026, eligibility for recruitment & trials of candidates,
having sports achievement certificates for the events concluded prior to 01.08.2026 for revised Weight
categories shall be decided as per the corresponding new weight categories, as detailed below.
