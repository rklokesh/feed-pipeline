Information regarding the city of examination and Admission
Certificate for the candidates of Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level
Examination, 2026 (Computer Based Examination) (Tier-I).
Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, 2026 (CBE) (Tier-I) is scheduled to
be held from 03.12.2026 to 31.12.2026. The candidates can view their examination city details
by logging in through the designated login module on the website of the Commission
(https://ssc.gov.in/).
2. The ‘Admission Certificate’ for the said examination will tentatively be available
for download prior to two/three (02/03) days of respective examination date. The same
can be accessed through the designated login module on the website of the commission
in a similar manner. Detailed instructions in this regard are available in the Notice
published on 07.09.2026 on the website of the Commission.
3. The candidates availing the ‘Own Scribe’ facility may require doing fresh Scribe
registration on the Commission’s website, as the registration of scribes prior to
05.11.2025 were disposed off.
4. The Scribe registration has now been integrated with the Aadhaar Authentication.
Therefore, it is mandatory that all such personnel, willing to enrol/ appear as a scribe,
may complete their fresh registration on the Commission’s website.
5. Candidates who are willing to avail the ‘Own Scribe’ facility in examination will be
required to submit their Scribe’s registration number for generation of their Scribe’s Entry
Pass. It has been decided by the Commission that the maximum permissible age limit of
personnel appearing as own Scribe will be as per the updated policy No. PP001/14/2024-
PP (e-10280) dated 04.11.2025. Rest of the guidelines regarding the Scribe facility will
remain same as per the notice of the examination.
6. Candidates who have opted for ‘own scribe’ will be able to map their Scribe OTR
till 28.11.2026 (11:00 P.M.). Scribe Entry Pass will be generated only for those
candidates who successfully map their Scribe OTR.
