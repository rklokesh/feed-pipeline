1. The National Testing Agency (NTA) is conducting the UGC-NET December 2026 Examination
for determining the eligibility of Indian nationals for the award of Junior Research Fellowship
(JRF), appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to Ph.D. programmes in Indian
Universities and Colleges.
2. In accordance with the Information Bulletin and the earlier Public Notices, candidates are being
provided an opportunity to correct particulars in their Online Application Forms for UGC-NET
December 2026 as per the schedule below:

3. All registered candidates are advised to visit the official website https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in and
verify the particulars in their respective Online Application Forms. Corrections, if required, may
be made only in the permitted fields during the above period.
4. This correction facility is being provided only once. Candidates are therefore advised to make
corrections carefully. No further opportunity for correction will be provided, and no correction
will be accepted after 04 November 2026 (11:50 P.M.).
5. Candidates shall be permitted to submit corrections only once. Once the corrected application
form is submitted, it shall be frozen and no further changes will be permitted.
6. Any additional fee, if applicable, shall be paid by the candidate concerned through Credit/Debit
Card, Net Banking or UPI only.
7. For further clarification regarding UGC-NET December 2026, candidates may contact the NTA
Helpdesk at 011-40759000 or write to ugcnet@nta.ac.in.
