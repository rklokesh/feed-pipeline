OFFICE MEMORANDUM

Subject: Procedure for issuance of Duplicate Scorecard to candidates of examinations conducted by
the National Testing Agency, regarding.

1. Preamble

1.1 The National Testing Agency (hereinafter "NTA") conducts, on behalf of the Government of India,
several national-level examinations, including the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test
(Undergraduate) [NEET-UG], JEE (Main), CUET (UG/PG), UGC-NET, CSIR-NET and such other
examinations as may be entrusted to it from time to time. Scorecards of candidates are generated
electronically and made available for download at the time of declaration of results.

1.2 The Information Bulletins issued in respect of such examinations have hitherto provided that the
record of the examination is preserved only for a specified period (typically 60 to 90 days) from the
date of declaration of results ("the retention clause"),

1.3 NTA has been receiving representations from candidates of past examination cycles seeking a duly
verified copy of their scorecard, principally from candidates of NEET-UG who have pursued
primary medical qualification abroad and now require the said document for the Foreign Medical
Graduate Examination (FMGE) conducted by the National Board of Examinations in Medical
Sciences (NBEMS) and for registration with the National Medical Commission (NMC) under the
Screening Test Regulations, 2002 (as amended), and analogous requirements for other
examinations.

1.4 No standing procedure has hitherto been prescribed for issuance of a duplicate scorecard in such
cases. In order to address this gap, and with the approval of the competent authority, the following
procedure is hereby notified with immediate effect.

2. Definitions

2.1 “Duplicate Scorecard" means a scorecard, verified from NTA's records and issued by NTA under
this Office Memorandum, in lieu of the scorecard originally generated at the time of declaration of
results. It is the only nomenclature to be used in all correspondence, applications, formats and
public communications under this Office Memorandum; expressions such as "Certificate of Result
Data", "verified scorecard", or "score statement" shall not be used.

2.2 “Applicant" means the candidate to whom the original scorecard was issued, or, in the case of a
deceased or legally incapacitated candidate, the legal heir or authorised representative applying
under paragraph 12.

2.3 “Competent Authority" means the Director General, NTA, or such officer as the Director General
may authorise in writing.

3. Applicability

3.1 This Office Memorandum shall apply to all examinations conducted by NTA in respect of which
the underlying result data is preserved in NTA's records and is not otherwise available to the
candidate through DigiLocker.

3.2 Notwithstanding the retention clause referred to in paragraph 1.2, this Office Memorandum shall
be applied to all applications where the result data is, in fact, available in NTA's records. Where
such data is not available, the Competent Authority shall issue a reasoned certificate to that effect
within the timeline prescribed in paragraph 8, and no application fee shall be retained.

3.3 NTA shall, in parallel, undertake to progressively onboard historical scorecards of eligible
examinations onto DigiLocker in a phased manner. The procedure prescribed under this Office
Memorandum shall operate as a residual channel and shall cease to apply to any examination and
year in respect of which the scorecard has been made available through DigiLocker.

4. Eligibility

4.1 An Applicant may apply for a Duplicate Scorecard under this Office Memorandum only where the
original scorecard has been (a) lost, (b) stolen, (c) mutilated or damaged, or (d) destroyed by fire,
flood, or other cause beyond the Applicant's control.

4.2 A Duplicate Scorecard shall be issued only in respect of the Applicant's own examination record. A
second or subsequent Duplicate Scorecard for the same examination and year shall be issued only
where the Competent Authority is satisfied, on the basis of documentary evidence, that the earlier

Duplicate Scorecard has also been lost, stolen, mutilated, damaged or destroyed.

5. Documents to be enclosed with the application

5.1 Every application under this Office Memorandum shall be accompanied by the following
documents, in original or self-attested photocopy as indicated:

(a) a duly filled and signed application in the form prescribed at Annexure 1;

(b) an affidavit in the form prescribed at Annexure II, sworn on non-judicial stamp paper of the
value prescribed in the State of execution (not less than Rs. 10/-), attested by a First-Class
Judicial Magistrate or a Notary Public duly appointed under the Notaries Act, 1952, declaring
the fact and circumstances of loss/theft/mutilation/damage/ destruction, and undertaking
that if the original scorecard is subsequently recovered, the same shall be surrendered to NTA;
(c) a copy of the First Information Report (FIR), Daily Diary Report (DDR), Non-Cognizable
Report (NCR) or lost-article report registered with the police in whose jurisdiction the loss or
theft is stated to have occurred; or, where such report cannot be obtained (including in cases of
loss abroad), an attestation by the Indian Embassy / Consulate of the country of residence to that
effect;

(d) two (2) recent passport-size colour photographs of the Applicant, one affixed on the
application form and one on the affidavit, both attested by a Gazetted Officer or the head of the
institution last attended;

(९) a self-attested photocopy of a government-issued photo identity document (Aadhaar, PAN,
Passport, Voter ID or Driving Licence), together with a self-attested photocopy of the Aadhaar
(with masked digits, in accordance with UIDAI guidelines) or, in the case of an Applicant
residing abroad, a self-attested photocopy of the passport;
