PUBLIC NOTICE
Conduct of 22nd Edition of Central Teacher Eligibility Test
It is hereby informed that the 22nd edition of the Central Teacher Eligibility Test
(CTET) will be conducted on 12th and 13th December 2026.
Information regarding the Examination City and the date of examination allotted to
each candidate will be made available on the CTET portal 15 days before the examination.
The Admit Card, containing details of the Examination Centre, will be made available for
download from the CTET portal two days before the examination.
