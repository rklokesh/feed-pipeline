The NTA has been entrusted by the University Grants Commission (UGC) with the task of
conducting UGC-NET, which is a test to determine the eligibility of Indian nationals for (i)
‘award of Junior Research Fellowship and appointment as Assistant Professor’, (ii)‘appointment
as Assistant Professor and admission to Ph.D.’ and (iii)‘admission to Ph.D. only’ in Indian
universities and colleges.
The National Testing Agency (NTA) will conduct UGC-NET December 2026 for 87 subjects in
