Public Notice

Subject: Extension of Last Date for Submission of Online Application Form for UGC-NET
December 2026 Examination – Reg.
1. The National Testing Agency (NTA) is conducting the UGC-NET December 2026 Examination for
determining the eligibility of Indian nationals for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF),
appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to Ph.D. programmes in Indian Universities and
Colleges.
2. In interest of candidates, it has been decided to extend the last date for submission of the Online
Application Form for UGC-NET December 2026 Examination up to 31 October 2026 (11:50 PM) without
any late fee to facilitate candidates who have not yet completed their application process. The revised
schedule is as follows:
Activity Revised Date
Last date for submission of Online Application Form 31 October 2026 (up to 11:50 PM)
Last date for successful transaction of examination fee 31 October 2026 (up to 11:50 PM)
3. Candidates are advised to complete their application process well before the revised deadline to avoid
any last-minute technical difficulties.
4. All other terms and conditions mentioned in the Information Bulletin and earlier Public Notices shall
remain unchanged.
5. Candidates are advised to regularly visit the official NTA websites https://nta.ac.in and
https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in for the latest updates.
