General instructions:
1.The recruitments will be purely on contractual basis and the selected candidates will have
no claim forregularization of the service in the ESIC hospital Korba .
2. Candidates seeking Reservation / Age relaxation benefits under SC/ST/OBC (NCL)
category must ensure that they are entitled to such reservation as per eligibility prescribed in
the notice and as per the instruction issued by Government of India. They should also be in
possession of the certificates in the prescribed format of Govt. of India in support of their
claim. Candidates seeking reservation under EWS category, should submit their EWS
certificate as prescribed vide Govt.of India, Department of Personnel & Training OM No.
36039/1/2019-Estt.(Res) dated 31-01-2019. Candidates claiming reservation/age relaxation
under OBC (NCL) candidates are required to submit latest OBC Certificate as per Central
Govt. Proforma (not more than one year old) from the date of Interview.
3. If no eligible candidates are available against the respective categories mentioned as
above, then any candidate with suitable qualifications may be considered for walk-in-
interview irrespective of category, for engaging Contractual Specialist for 39 days extendable
by another 39 days subject to fulfillment of Essential Qualifications and other conditions.
against reserved posts for SC, ST & OBC as per order No.36036/3/2018 Estt. (Res.) dated
15.05.2018 of Govt. of India. The contract will be renewable with further advertisement in
absence of reserved candidates.
4. Private practices of any kind will not be allowed for full time contractual Specialist.
5. ESIC may increase or decrease or cancel filling up of any or all the post without assigning
any reasons at the discretion of the Appointing Authority. In case of any discrepancy, All
the terms and conditions related with the walk in interview will be valid for and compiled
with as per this latest advertisement will be final, thereafter if any issue still persist ,
decision of Medical Superintendent ESICH Korba will be final.
6. No TA/DA will be paid to the candidates for appearing in the interview.
7. Application should be submitted in the prescribed form appended below. It should be filled
up in block/capital letters by the candidate in his/her own handwriting.
8. Incomplete or Unsigned applications or the applications received without the self-attested
copies of mark sheets/certificates in support of educational qualification, proof of age (Birth
certificate or class 10th marksheet ), application fee, caste certificate, experience certificates,
photographs and those received after the last date of receipt of applications are liable to be
rejected without any communication to the candidate.
9. ESIC will not undertake detailed scrutiny of applications for eligibility and other aspects
for calling candidates for interview and, therefore, the candidature will be accepted only
provisionally. The candidates fulfilling the eligibility criteria for the post will be called for
interview on the basis of information furnished by them in the application form. Mere
submission of application does not confer any right to the candidates for being called for
interview. Before applying, candidates are advised to go through the requirements of
essential qualification, age, experience etc. and satisfy themselves that they are eligible for
the post. At the time of verification/submission of documents on the day of interview, if any
claim made in the application is not found substantiated, the candidature of the candidate
will be cancelled and the decision of MS ESIC hospital Korba in this regard shall be final.
10. Wrong declarations/submission of false information or any other action contrary to law
shall lead to cancellation of the candidature at any stage.
11. Hostel accommodation/ quarters will not be provided.
12. The candidate who is in service should submit “No Objection Certificate” from the
employer at the time ofinterview.
13. The posts are contractual and the contractual engagement may be terminated/
discontinued by either sideby giving one month’s prior notice before resigning or will have to
pay an amount equivalent to one month’s remuneration in lieu of the notice period.
14. The number of vacancies may be changed without prior notice. The decision of the
Medical Superintendent will be final on all aspects of selection and no further
correspondence willbe entertained under any circumstances.
15. In case of selection, OFFER OF APPOINTMENT will be issued as per requirement.
16. The selected candidate will be required to deposit security money of ₹30000/- (Rupees
Thirty thousand only) through Demand Draft in favor of “ESI Fund Account No.1”, Korba at
the time of joining. The same is refundable after completion of contract period and on
production of “No dues certificate”. If the selected candidate resigns/terminates his/her
contract without prior notice, the amount deposited as security money will be forfeited.
17. If any candidate wants to work as part time contractual specialist for a post advertised
for full time contractual, it can be done at discretion of MS but he will have to see
emergencies on call basis for which he will be paid ₹15000/- per month as per decision of
Medical Superintendent.
18. Qualifications with regard to experience are relaxable at the discretion of the Appointing
Authority on recommendation of the Selection Committee in case of candidates found
otherwise well qualified.
19. The Qualifications regarding experience in respect of Scheduled Castes and the
Scheduled Tribes candidates is relaxable based on the guidelines issued by the Government
of India from time to time.
20. Work experience after obtaining the P.G. degree/ diploma will be reckoned as on the
date of interview. Experience certificates duly signed by the Head of Department indicating
clearly the period and nature of work, should be submitted along with the application form.
21. The MBBS or equivalent qualification and Post Graduate Qualification should be
recognized by the MCI/NMC/State Medical /Dental Council Chhattishgarh. The candidates are
advised to attach the certificate of registration/recognition of their MBBS or equivalent
qualification and Post Graduate Qualification with the MCI/NMC/State Medical Council
Chhattisgarh. The candidate whose MBBS or equivalent and Post Graduate Qualification is
not recognized by the MCI/ NMC/State Medical Council Chhattisgarh, he/she will not be
eligible for the above post.
22. The selected Full Time/Part Time Specialist will be required to submit certificate of
medical fitness from Government Medical Hospital with issuing authority not below rank of
Civil surgeon or Assistant Medical Superintendent.
23. The selected candidate must have a professional indemnity policy which should cover
the period as desired byESIC. The policy is meant to cover the professional liability falling on
them as a result of errors and omissions committed by them while rendering professional
services. The minimum sum assured per annum should be
a) Rs 30 lakhs for Anesthesiologists, Cardiac Surgeons, Neuro Surgeons, Plastic
Surgeons etc.
b) Rs 20 lakhs for General Surgeons, Obstetrics&Gynecology etc.
c) Rs 10 lakhs for Physicians , Dentists, Radiologists, Pathologists, Chest Physicians
(Pulmonologists) etc.
A copy of the Insurance & Premium Receipt must be submitted within 7 days from the
date of engagement.
24. Absence from work for a period of 15 days without proper permission of competent
authority; will amount to voluntary abandonment of engagement and no further order of
disengagement shall be necessary.
25. Selected candidate will have to sign contract of agreement on bond paper of ₹100/-.The
cost of bond paper should be borne by selected candidate only.
26. All the terms and conditions related with the walk in interview will be valid for and
compiled with as per this latest advertisement.
