Public Notice
01.11.2026
Subject: Extension of Last Date for Successful Transaction of Examination Fee for UGC-NET
December 2026 Examination – Reg.
1. The National Testing Agency (NTA) is conducting the UGC-NET December 2026 Examination for
determining the eligibility of Indian nationals for the award of Junior Research Fellowship (JRF),
appointment as Assistant Professor, and admission to Ph.D. programmes in Indian Universities and
Colleges.
2. Vide Public Notice dated 27.10.2026, the last date for submission of the Online Application Form and for
successful transaction of the examination fee for UGC-NET December 2026 Examination was extended
up to 31 October 2026 (11:50 PM).
3. In interest of candidates who have completed and submitted their Online Application Forms but could
not complete the fee payment within the stipulated timeline, it has been decided to extend the last date
for successful transaction of the examination fee by one additional day. The revised schedule is as
follows:
Activity Revised Date
Last date for successful transaction of examination fee
(only for candidates who have already submitted their 01 November 2026 (up to 11:50 PM)
Online Application Form)
4. There is no extension for registration, submission of the Online Application Form, or upload of images;
these activities have closed on 31 October 2026 (11:50 PM). This extension is applicable only to the
payment of the examination fee by candidates whose application forms have already been submitted.
5. Candidates are advised to complete the fee payment well before the revised deadline and to download
and retain the Confirmation Page generated after successful payment.
6. All other terms and conditions mentioned in the Information Bulletin and earlier Public Notices shall
remain unchanged.
7. Candidates are advised to regularly visit the official NTA websites https://nta.ac.in and
https://ugcnet.nta.nic.in for the latest updates.
