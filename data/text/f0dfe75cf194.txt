Terms and Conditions:
1. Application in the prescribed format, complete in all respects (Entries and
the documents required to be enclosed), with Photograph affixed on the
application, should reach this office on or before 28.11.2026 (Saturday)
by 4 pm through e-mail only at principal-
nursingindiranagar.ka@esic.gov.in. Please note that applications that are
incomplete in any respect will be disqualified.
2. Eligible candidates shortlisted for interview will be intimated on 01.12.2026
through e-mail and through the ESIC website to appear for interview in person on
03.12.2026 at 8.40 am.
3. Date, Time and Venue of Interview: 03.12.2026 from 8.40 am at
ACADEMIC BLOCK, ESIC Medical College, PGIMSR & Model Hospital,
Rajajinagar, Bengaluru–560010.
NOTE: Candidates reporting after 11.00 am will not be considered for interview.
4. Date and Time of Interview: 03.12.2026 from 08.40 am onwards.
5. Engagement of visiting/part-time Lecturers on an hourly basis shall be for the
respective semesters of the Academic Year 2026-27.
6. The crucial date for determining the Age limit, work experience and other criteria
regarding eligibility for the post shall be the date of interview, that is 03.12.2026.
7. The Recruitment is purely on an hourly basis, and this engagement can be
terminated/ discontinued on either side by giving one-month prior notice to this
effect without assigning any reasons.
8. Other terms & conditions will be applicable as issued from the Competent Authority
from time to time.
9. No TA /DA shall be payable to any candidate for appearing in the Interview.
10. No other benefits/facilities will be claimed by the Visiting/part-time lecturer
engaged, and the same will not be granted to them either.
11. Candidates must meet the requisite criteria for eligibility.
12. No interim correspondence/enquiry will be entertained.
13. Any false/fabricated information will lead to disqualification.
14. Competent Authority of ESIC reserves the right to engage or not to engage for any
post.
15. Competent Authority, ESIC, reserves the right to alter the date / venue or cancel the
engagement process at any stage at its discretion, and such decision shall be
binding on all concerned.
16. Selection Procedure:
A. Selection will be purely based on performance in interviews conducted by the
No/532/NURSINGCOLLEGE/PARTTIMELECTURERS/2013. I/4632645/2026
selection board. The candidate will be required to present one short (5
minutes) PowerPoint presentation related to the respective subject;
hence, one should come prepared and bring the PPT on a pen drive. The
selected candidates will be informed through email, and the list will be published on
the ESIC website; no query or correspondence will be entertained in this regard.
B. Documents to be submitted (Two sets of self-attested Photocopies clearly Legible)
along with application through mail to principal-nursingindiranagar.ka@esic.gov.in
& Originals to be produced for verification on the day of interview COMPULSORILY
as follows:
I. The original set of the application form, complete in all respects (Entries and the
documents required to be enclosed), with Photograph affixed to the application, to
be submitted on the interview day. Please note that applications that are
incomplete in any respect will be disqualified.
II. Proof of Date of Birth - SSLC / 10th Standard Certificate or equivalent.
III. Certificates of qualifications (Degree certificates & Year-wise Marks list - UG & PG).
IV. Experience Certificates.
V. AADHAAR Card copy.
VI. Two passport-size photographs with the application.
C. As this is an hourly-based engagement, the selected candidates will be informed of
the date of commencement of the classes to be conducted.
Note: The candidate may contact the Principal, ESIC College of Nursing,
Indiranagar, Bengaluru, on Phone No. : 080-29517958 / 9738643553 between
11.30 AM to 04.00 PM on working days for any clarification. They may also
send their relevant queries, if any, by email:principal-
nursingindiranagar.ka@esic.gov.in
