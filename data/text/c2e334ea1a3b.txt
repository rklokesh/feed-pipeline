Subject - Information regarding the city of examination and admission certificate for the
candidates of Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces
Examination, 2026 (Paper-I).
As per notices published by the Commission on its website, Paper-I of Sub-Inspector
in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination, 2026 is scheduled from 23rd
Nov. 2026 to 28th Nov. 2026
2. The candidates can view their examination city details and admission certificate by
logging in through the designated login module on the website of the Commission
(https://ssc.gov.in/). The ‘Admission Certificate’ will tentatively be available for download,
prior to 2-3 days of respective examination date. Detailed instructions in this regard are
available in the concerned notices of examination.
3. Certain discrepancies have been noticed in the details of matriculation qualification of
approximately 21,000 candidates of Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police
Forces Examination, 2026 (Paper-I). Though these candidates are provisionally being
permitted to appear in the examination, they are required to upload a copy of their Class 10
marksheet by login to the candidate portal on ssc.gov.in and visit City Live / Admit Card Status
page for SICPO 2026 examination before 20th November 2026 to avoid any inconvenience
during result processing. In this connection, such candidates are also being contacted over
email by the respective SSC Regional Offices.
