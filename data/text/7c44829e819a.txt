ACCEPTABILITY OF PERMANENT BODY TATTOO(S) FOR
RECRUITMENT IN INDIAN COAST GUARD
1. Acceptability of Tattoo(s). The policy regarding acceptability of permanent body
tattoos of candidates aspiring to join the Indian Coast Guard is amplified in the succeeding
paragraphs:-
(a) Location of Tattoo(s). Permanent body tattoos are only permitted on inner
face of forearms i.e. from inside of elbow to the wrist and on the reverse side of palm/
back (dorsal) side of hand. Permanent body tattoo on any other part of the body is not
acceptable and candidate will be barred from recruitment.
(b) Size of Tattoo(s). Only small innocuous tattoos that are not prejudicial to
good order and military discipline are permitted e.g. religious symbols or names of near
and dear ones.
(c) Nature of Tattoo(s). A tattoo must not have any lewd or offensive indelible
marks or figure on any part of the body. Tattoos that are indecent (grossly offensive
to modesty, decency, or propriety), sexist (demean a person based on gender) or racist
(degrade or demean a person based on race ethnicity or region and religion).
(d) Exception of Tattoo(s) on Candidates From Tribal Communities.
Candidates from tribal communities/ from tribal areas as declared by the Government
of India Scheduled Tribes Orders/ Act/ Lists (amended and modified from time to time),
are permitted to have permanent body tattoos on any part of the body as per existing
customs and traditions of the said tribe to which a candidate belongs.
2. Candidates with permanent body tattoo(s) will be permitted to continue with the
selection/ recruitment process in the Indian Coast Guard only on the condition that the
following certification are submitted by the candidate at the time of medical examination at
Stage II and Stage III of the recruitment process:-
(a) For all candidates. A self-certification signed by the individual declaring
that the candidate will not have any additional tattoos in future as per format at
Appendix `A’ below.
(b) Additional Certification for candidates from Tribal Community.
Certificate of belonging to a tribal community and permissibility of permanent body
tattoo(s) as per format placed at Appendix `B’ signed by any of the following officials:-
(i) District Collector/ District Magistrate or Sub-District Magistrate of the
District/ Tehsil where the tribe of the candidate is presently settled, or
(ii) Chairman/ Secretary or any other Senior Member of the tribe.
3. Procedure for medical examination of Candidates with Tattoo(s). The
following procedure is adopted for medical examination of candidates with permanent
tattoo(s): -
(a) All candidates having permanent body tattoos are required to render certificate
as per para 2 above.
(b) The decision of Indian Coast Guard will be final and binding on the candidate
regarding acceptability of permanent body tattoo(s) for recruitment in Indian Coast
Guard.
