Subject: Corrigendum to the Information Bulletin of UGC-NET December 2026
Examination - reg.

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has reviewed the Information Bulletin issued for the UGC-
NET December 2026 Examination and has made certain amendments, corrections and clarifications
to its provisions, with a view to ensuring greater clarity, consistency and transparency for
candidates.

2. Accordingly, a consolidated corrigendum containing the amendments and clarifications to
the existing Information Bulletin is hereby issued. The details of the existing provisions and
the corresponding revised provisions are provided in the Comparative Statement enclosed
as Annexure-A to this Public Notice.

3. Further, the Updated Information Bulletin for UGC-NET December 2026, incorporating the
aforesaid amendments and clarifications, is also being issued and enclosed as Annexure-B
for the information and guidance of all concerned.

4. Candidates are advised to carefully go through the Comparative Statement (Annexure-A)
and the Updated Information Bulletin (Annexure-B) before filling in their application forms
and appearing in the examination.

5. The amendments and clarifications contained in Annexure-A shall form an integral part of
the Information Bulletin for UGC-NET December 2026. In the event of any inconsistency
between the earlier Information Bulletin and the Updated Information Bulletin, the
provisions of the Updated Information Bulletin shall prevail.

6. All other terms and conditions, instructions and provisions of the Information Bulletin,
which have not been amended through this corrigendum, shall remain unchanged.

7. Candidates are advised to regularly visit the official websites of NTA (https://nta.ac.in) and
UGC-NET (https://ugenet.nta.ac.in) for the latest updates and announcements regarding

the examination.
