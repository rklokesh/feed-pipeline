FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQs)
Recruitment for Isolated Categories under CEN No. 09/2026
(English)
01. What is the opening date of application for CEN No. 09/2026?
The opening date of application is 05.11.2026.
02. What is the closing date for submission of Application for CEN No.
09/2026?
The closing date for submission of online application is 04.12.2026 @23.59Hrs.
And the last date for payment of the application fee for submitted applications is
06.12.2026.
03. What are the dates for modification of application?
Modification window will open only after closing date for submission of
application and will be active from 07.12.2026 to 16.12.2026. If candidates wish
to modify, change or correct any details except details filled in the ‘Create an
Account’ form (including email id and mobile number) and chosen RRB, they
may do so by paying a modification fee of Rs.250/- (non-refundable) for each
occasion. (Please Note: Details filled in ‘Create an Account’ form and ‘Chosen
RRB’ cannot be modified).
04. My final results are expected after closing date for submission of
application. Can I apply?
No, Candidates waiting for final results of prescribed educational/technical
qualifications are not eligible to apply. Candidates should possess prescribed
educational/technical qualifications on or before the closing date for submission
of application for this CEN.
05. What are the requirements to ‘Create an Account’?
Candidates should have their own active mobile number and a valid personal
email ID for communication during the entire duration of recruitment, as
communication with them will be through SMS and/or email.
06. I have created an account for previous CENs of railways. Should I
create another account for CEN 09/2026?
NO, Candidates who have already created an account during any notification
published by Railway Recruitment Board in the year 2024/2025/2026 are not
required to Create an Account again. They should login using the same
username and password to apply for CEN No. 09/2026.

07. What is the procedure to ‘Create an Account’?
If a candidate has not yet created an account on the RRB application portal, they
must first create an account before proceeding to fill up the application for this
CEN. To do so, click on the "Apply" tab on the application link home page and
then select "Create an Account". Fill in the required details in the online account
creation form and create a password. The password should contain a
combination of capital letters, small letters, numbers and special characters.
Upon successful “Creation of the Account”, a confirmation SMS and email will be
sent to the candidate.
08. Can I edit the ‘Create an Account’ details?
Candidates CANNOT edit any details of the ‘Create an account’ once the
account is created.
09. What is next procedure after ‘Create an Account’?
• After Create an Account, Log-in to the application in rrbapply.gov.in portal
using RRB credentials or AADHAAR credentials.
• There are five broad sections to complete the online submission of
application like Personal Details, Other Details, Educational Qualification,
Upload Profile Documents, Preview and Preference & Submit.
• The details filled in “Create an Account” stage will pre-populate in the
application which cannot be edited. Begin by entering all details, starting
with Personal Details. After entering personal details, save and proceed to
the next section and continue in this manner until you reach the last section
i.e., Preview and Preference & Submit, where a candidate can finally
submit the online application for payment.
• After completion of fee payment process, the applicant will receive a
confirmation on successful payment through email & SMS from RRB. After
that, a candidate can take a printout of the submitted application containing
the registration number.

then proceed further for submission of application for payment. Details filled

12. How many Pay Levels are there in CEN 09/2026?
There are three pay level in CEN No. 09/2026 (Level-2, Level-6 & Level-7).
13. How to apply?
Kindly refer to Para 14.0 of CEN for details on how to apply. Candidates must

Candidates are advised to note and remember their Registration Number of

15. What is the crucial date for PwBD certificate?
PwBD candidates must possess their disability certificate on or before closing

16. Can I apply to different RRBs for same pay levels?

17. Can I apply to more than one RRB?
No, you should not. Applying to more than one RRB will result in the rejection of
all applications and will lead to debarment from all future examinations of RRB
and RRC.
18. Can I apply multiple applications to same RRB?
No, you should not. Multiple applications to same RRB will result in the rejection
of all applications and will lead to debarment from all future examinations of RRB
and RRC.
19. I have undergone LASIK surgery. Am I eligible to apply?
Candidate’s undergone LASIK surgery is eligible for posts that require Medical
Standards B1, C1 & C2. Please refer Note (c) of Para 3.0 of CEN No. 09/2026.
20. Why DigiLocker/Aadhaar Verification is required?
Digi Locker/Aadhaar validation is required for smoother process at different
stages of recruitment for the candidates themselves. Further, to ensure
genuineness of candidature and to curb impersonation during CBTs. RRBs will
not share Aadhaar details with any third party or use them for any purpose other
than the objectives of this CEN. If applicants verify their identity & other primary
details with alternate Photo IDs, they will be subjected to significantly stricter &
more detailed scrutiny at every stage of recruitment process.
21. What should I do, if I don’t have Aadhaar?
Candidates are advised, in their own interest, to authenticate their identity using
Digilocker or Aadhaar during the "Create an Account" stage. If they missed this
stage, they can still authenticate during the online application details filling
process by using the “Verify Aadhaar" facility and providing their Aadhaar details.
This must be completed before final submission of application for a particular
CEN. This will facilitate a smoother process at different stages of recruitment for
the candidates themselves. If applicants verify their identity & other primary
details with alternate Photo IDs, they will be subjected to significantly stricter &
more detailed scrutiny at every stage of recruitment process.
22. What document should I produce if I have already changed my name?
Applicants must possess a Gazette Notification or any other legal document as
applicable for name changes and should be submitted at the time of CBTs & DV.
Date of such change or application for change should be prior to the date of
submission of online application. Applicants should also have to fill the original
name and the changed name during ‘Create an Account’. In such cases, both the
names will be reflected in the application.

23. What should I do if I forgot password to login My Account?
Click on the “Forgot Password” option in the “Sign into Your Account” section of
the login page. Enter either Aadhaar number or registered email ID/mobile
number and date of birth. The revised password will be sent to the candidate’s
registered email ID only. Candidates have to login with the revised password
sent to their registered email ID.
24. Can I change my password?
Yes. Candidates should first login by using the old password. After logging in,
Candidates can change their old password by accessing the 'Change Password'
option available on the upper right- hand side of the online application page.
25. Can I change my ‘Chosen RRB’?
No, you cannot change the ‘chosen RRB’ after submission of application.
26. Can I edit my details during the application process?
Yes. Any details in the application other than the details entered during ‘Create
an Account’ can be edited till the application is submitted and registration number
generated.

28. Can I edit my application details after submission of online application
before making payment?

preview at Preference and Preview & Submit page for correctness and further
submit application if all the information is correct since no change is permitted
after submission of application for payment. If any information is found
incorrect at application preview, they can click on ‘Edit’ button and rectify the
incorrect information at respective page of application module, then again
preview application for correctness and submit the application.

29. Who are eligible for ‘Free Travel Authority’ (free Railway Pass)?
Free Sleeper Class Railway Pass admissible to SC & ST candidates (if opted in
application) will be provided with their City Intimation Slip/e-call letter for various
stages of selection viz., CBT/DV/Medical Examination (as applicable) as per the
details and valid caste certificate furnished in their ONLINE application. At the
Community section of the Personal Details page, they can avail the same by
choosing the ‘Yes’ option from the drop-down under ‘Do you wish to avail free rail
travel pass for the exam’ and fill in ‘Nearest Railway Station’. Only candidates
who wish to avail the free rail travel pass will be asked to upload their Caste
Certificate. Please refer to Para 19.0 (e) of the detailed CEN for complete
information
30. How do I avail ‘Free Travel Authority’ (free Railway Pass)?
While booking train reservation and travelling, the original SC & ST caste
certificate must be shown to the authorized Railway staff concerned, for
verification of the genuineness of the candidate. They will be allowed to book
train reservation by submitting a self-attested photocopy of City Intimation Slip/e-
call letter and SC/ST certificate at the ticket booking counter. SC/ST candidates
should carry their original community certificate and an original proof of identity
for undertaking journey, failing which they will be treated as travelling without
ticket and will be charged accordingly. Any misuse of this free travel facility shall
lead to rejection of candidature at any stage of the selection process, including
debarment from future examinations. Please refer to Para 19.0 (e) of the detailed
CEN for complete information.
31. How can I receive the free travel facility?
In case of eligible SC & ST candidates, their City Intimation Slip/e-call letter itself
will contain the free travel authority (sleeper class railway pass). They will be
allowed to book train reservation by submitting a self-attested photocopy of their
City Intimation Slip/e- call letter and SC/ST certificate at the ticket booking
counter. For further details, refer Para 19.0 (e) of CEN No. 09/2026

32. I am unable to upload my Caste Certificate.
Check whether the scanned document is in ‘PDF’ format and the size is less than
400 KB.
33. What is EBC?
EBC is ‘Economically Backward Class’. Economically Backward Class (EBC)
candidates are those whose annual family income is less than Rs.50,000/-.
Candidates should not confuse Economically Backward Class with other
Backward Class (OBC) or Economically Weaker Section (EWS) or Extremely
Backward Class. Refer para 7.3
34. What are the benefits given to EBC candidates?
EBC candidates are given ‘Fee concession’ only but they are not eligible for
reservation benefits.
35. What will happen if a candidate failed to produce EBC Certificate?
If a candidate availed fee concession as EBC, he/she should have a valid
Income Certificate (as per Annexure III A of the CEN) or BPL Card or any other
certificate issued by Central Government under the recognized poverty
alleviation program or IZZAT MST issued by Railways before submission of
online application and will require to submit at the time of Document Verification,
failing which his/her candidature will be rejected. Refer Para 7.3 of detailed CEN
for complete information.
36. Are there reserved posts for EBC?
No. EBC candidates are eligible only for fee concession. There is no reservation
of posts for EBC candidates.
37. Who is included under minorities?
Minorities include Muslims, Christians, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains and Zoroastrians
(Parsis) which were given minority status by the State/UT where the candidate
belongs, subject to evasion/deletion/inclusion if any received till last date of
submission of online application (please refer Para 7.2 of CEN No. 09/2026).
38. What are the benefits given to Minorities candidates? What will happen
if a candidate failed to produce an
affidavit for minority community declaration during document verification?

43. What is the crucial date to claim SC/ST/OBC/EWS status?
Community /category status for SC, ST, OBC-NCL and EWS as on the closing
date of submission of applications against this CEN shall only be considered for
reservation and related benefits as per eligibility. Any change in this status there
after shall not be considered. Please refer para 8.0 (Vertical Reservation) of the
detailed CEN for complete information. Candidates seeking reservation on the
basis of SC/ ST/ OBC/ EWS status must ensure that they possess the caste/
community certificate as on the closing date for submission of application of this
CEN. During DV, such candidates should produce their original caste/community
certificate (in prescribed format) valid as on the closing date for submission of
application.
