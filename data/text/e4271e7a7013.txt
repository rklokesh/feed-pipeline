Subject: Advisory for Candidates regarding Aadhaar Data Updates and
Biometric Authentication.
The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) invites attention of the candidates to
the advisories issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) regarding
Aadhaar authentication and biometric validation.
2. To ensure a seamless, secure, and expeditious identity verification in
examination process, all candidates are advised to adhere to the following directives:
• Update of Demographic and Biometric Details:
Candidates must ensure that their demographic particulars (such as name,
date of birth, and address) and biometric records (including fingerprints, iris scans,
and facial data) are updated.
• Permanent Unlocking Status Throughout Examination Duration:
UIDAI facilitates both temporary and permanent biometric unlocking options.
Candidates are hereby notified that a temporary unlock is valid for a duration of
10 minutes only, which is inadequate for the multi-tiered verification protocols
executed at examination centers. Accordingly, candidates must ensure that their
biometrics remain permanently unlocked for the entire duration of the examination
process, encompassing entry, biometric authentication, in-exam verification, and exit
procedures. Candidates may however, re-lock their biometric credentials subsequent
to the successful conclusion of the examination, if they so wish.
3. Candidates are strongly advised to complete all necessary modifications and
verify their Aadhaar authentication status well in advance to preclude any last-minute
administrative inconveniences.
