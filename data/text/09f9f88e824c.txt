UNION PUBLIC SERVICE COMMISSION
IMPORTANT NOTICE
CIVIL SERVICES EXAMINATION-2026
PERSONALITY TEST
Dated: 17.11.2026
On the basis of the result of the Civil Services (Main) Examination,
2026 declared by the Union Public Service Commission on 5th November,
2026, the Commission has decided to commence the Personality Tests
(Interviews) of the Civil Services (Main) Examination, 2026 w.e.f. Monday,
07.12.2026.
2. The P.T. schedule of 2526 candidates, indicating their Roll Number,
Date and Session of interview from 07.12.2026 to 12.02.2027, is given
below. Reporting Time for Forenoon Session is 09:00 HRS and for Afternoon
Session is 13:00 HRS.
3. The PT schedule has been prepared and uploaded in respect of all the
2526 candidates, who have qualified for PT for CSE, 2026. However, if any
candidate fails to fill up/update and submit the requisite details ONLINE on
the Commission’s website within the prescribed window, i.e., from 11th
November, 2026 to 25th November, 2026 till 6:00 P.M.; no e-Summon
letter will be issued to them and they will not be allowed to appear in
the PT.
4. The e-Summon Letters of Personality Tests (Interviews) of all the
eligible candidates will be made available shortly, which may be downloaded
from the Commission’s Website https://www.upsc.gov.in and
https://upsconline.nic.in. No request for change in the date and time of
the Personality Test (Interview) intimated to the candidates will
ordinarily be entertained.
5. The candidates appearing for Interview will be granted reimbursement
towards travelling expenses for appearing in the Interview which will be
restricted only to the Second/Sleeper class train fare (Mail Express). In
case, the candidates perform their journey by any other mode/class, the
same will be dealt as per S.R.-132 and the Commission’s guidelines,
available on the Commission’s website i.e. https://upsc.gov.in/forms-
downloads→ [Travelling Allowance Form for Candidates (For Candidates
only)]. The candidates will have to submit hard copies/Print out of both
ways tickets, showing the details of fare alongwith the prescribed T.A.
contribution claim form duly filled in duplicate. The TA bill forms are
available at https:/www.upsc.gov.in/forms-downloads.
