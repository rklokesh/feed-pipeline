Subject : Inviting Online Applications for SHRESHTA (NETS) - 2027

The National Testing Agency (NTA), on behalf of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment,
invites online applications for SHRESHTA (NETS) 2027 [Scheme for Residential Education for Students
in High Schools in Targeted Areas] examination. The SHRESHTA scheme aims to provide high-quality
education to meritorious Scheduled Caste (SC) and De-notified Tribes {DNT (not covered under SCs, STs
and OBCs} students both Boys & Girls in reputed Private Residential Schools. About 2400 (2160 SCs +
240 DNTs) students would be selected for admission in Class 9 and Class 11 under the scheme with the
entire cost of education funded by the Central Government.

2. The examination for the Academic Session 2027-28 is proposed to be conducted in 192 cities
across India through Pen-and-Paper Based Test (PBT) mode.
