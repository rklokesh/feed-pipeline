Computer Based Examination – regarding.
The result of Computer Based Examination in Constable (GD) in Central
Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles
Examination, 2026 has been declared by the Commission on 12.10.2026.
2. The Commission has uploaded the Final Answer Keys along with Question
Paper(s) cum Response Sheet(s) of Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces
(CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026 on the
website of the Commission on 03.11.2026.
3. The candidates may log in through the link provided below by using their Roll
Number and Password. This facility will be available for the candidates for a period
from 03.11.2026 (1700 Hrs.) to 17.11.2026 (1700 Hrs.).
4. Further, the marks of the candidates who appeared in Computer Based
Examination have also been made available on the Commission’s website i.e.
https://ssc.gov.in from 03.11.2026 (1700 Hrs.) to 17.11.2026 (1700 Hrs.) which can
be accessed by the candidates by logging-in through their Registered ID and Password
on the website of the Commission.
5. The candidates may take a printout of their respective Final Answer Keys along
with Question Paper cum Response Sheet as well as scorecard, as the same will not be
available after the above specified time limit.
