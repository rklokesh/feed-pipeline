Subject: Inviting Online Applications for the National Institute of Fashion Technology
Entrance Examination (NIFTEE)–2027 for Admissions to UG, PG, NLEA,
Artisans and Ph.D. Programmes-reg.
1. The National Testing Agency (NTA), on behalf of the National Institute of Fashion Technology
(NIFT), invites online applications for NIFTEE–2027 for admission to Undergraduate (UG),
Postgraduate (PG), NIFT Lateral Entry Admissions (NLEA), Artisans and Ph.D. programmes.
The examination for the Academic Session 2027–28 is proposed to be conducted in 100 cities
across India through Computer Based Test (CBT) and Pen-and-Paper Based Test (PBT) modes,
as applicable to the test component.
2. NIFTEE–2027 Stage 1 will cover admissions to (i) UG Programmes B.Des. and B.F.Tech.; (ii) PG
Programmes M.Des., M.F.M. and M.F.Tech.; (iii) NLEA B.Des. (Lateral Entry) and B.F.Tech.
(Lateral Entry); (iv) Artisans Category for B.Des. and (v) Ph.D. admissions. The applicable test
components and subsequent selection stages will be as prescribed in the NIFT Admissions
Guidelines–2027. Ph.D. candidates will be required to appear for a Written Test conducted by
NIFT, followed by a Research Proposal Presentation and Interview (RPPI) to be conducted by
NIFT.
3. Candidates must carefully check the eligibility requirements, including age, educational
qualifications, subject combinations (where applicable) and category-specific criteria mentioned
in the NIFT Admissions Guidelines–2027 and the NIFTEE–2027 Information Bulletin.
Candidates are strongly advised to read both documents carefully before applying.
NRI/Foreign National/OCI/PIO/SAARC category candidates may apply as per the
prescribed NIFT norms mentioned in the NIFT Admission Guidelines-2027.
4. Applications are to be submitted online only through the NTA examination portal. Candidates
must upload their recent photograph, signature and other applicable documents in the
prescribed format and size. Aadhaar-based e-KYC and a live photograph may be required
during the application process. PwD/PwBD candidates must upload a valid UDID Card in the
prescribed format.
