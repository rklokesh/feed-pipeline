Subject: Declaration of First Round of Tentative Allocation of Combined Hindi
Translators Examination, 2025 - reg.
Result of Paper-I of Combined Hindi Translators Examination, 2025 (CHTE-
2025) was declared on 04.11.2025 wherein a total of 3642 candidates were shortlisted for
appearing in Paper-II. Thereafter, Paper-II of examination was conducted on 14.12.2025.
Result of Paper-II to shortlist candidates for appearing in PET/PST for the post of Sub Inspector
(Hindi Translator) in Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF), was declared by the Commission
on 04.06.2026.
2. First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA) has been done in accordance with Notice
dated 05.06.2025, Corrigendum dated 18.07.2025, Addendum dated 27.06.2026 of Combined
Hindi Translators Examination, 2025 (CHTE-2025) and Notice dated 03.03.2026 on
introduction of Sliding Mechanism for qualifying candidates published on website of the
Commission.
3. In accordance with the Important Notice No. HQ-C11017/2/2025-C-1 dated
08.09.2026, Option-cum-preference has been taken online from the candidates who appeared
in Paper-II of CHTE-2025 from 08.09.2026 (23:00 hrs) to 11.09.2026 (23:00 hrs). A total of
2724 candidates who have submitted their preferences online have been considered for further
selection process.
4. As per Para 16.3 of Notice of Examination, minimum qualifying marks in Paper-II of
the examination has been fixed by the Commission as under:
i. UR: 30%
ii. OBC/EWS: 25%
iii. All Other Categories: 20%
5. Resolution of Tie Cases : As per Para 17 of the Notice of the Examination, in the
event of tie in scores of candidates in the examination, merit has been decided by applying
following criteria, one after another in the given order, till the tie is resolved:
i. Total marks in Paper-II.
ii. Total Marks in Paper-I.
iii. Date of birth, with older candidates placed higher.
iv. Alphabetical order of the names of the candidates.

11. In pursuance of Para 16.17 of Notice of examination, the Commission vide Notice
dated 03.03.2026, has introduced a „Sliding Mechanism‟ to ensure maximum filling of
vacancies in a given examination cycle, provide fair and merit-based opportunities to
candidates and minimize the unfilled vacancies arising from absenteeism of the candidates
who are opting for non-joining in the given examination cycle. The mechanism aims to
establish a single round of reallocation process for the unutilized vacancies.
Accordingly, the Sliding Process/Identity Verification of selected candidates in FRTA will be
conducted at the cities of the concerned Regional Offices on 18.11.2026. The candidates
can download their sliding process/identity verification slip by logging in through the
designated login module on the website of the Commission (https://ssc.gov.in/) with effect
from 13.11.2026 (05:00 PM onwards). Any candidate failing to attend the sliding process
physically will be treated as absent and will not be considered for the final result.
Recommended candidates of CHTE-2025 in FRTA are also advised to go through the Notice
dated 08.04.2026 on Sliding Mechanism and various notices issued in this regard on the
Commission‟s website.
12. Marks of the shortlisted/non-shortlisted candidates will be uploaded on the website of
the Commission after declaration of final result of the examination.
