Subject: Mandatory forwarding of lnternal Audit Reports through e-Office in addition to hard

Madam/Sir,
With reference to this office letter dated 13.03.2026 regardingthe mandatory use of e-Office
modules, it has been decided to streamline the process of submission and forwarding of lnternal
Audit Reports of Kendriya Vidyalayas.
Accordingly, all Regional Offices are requested to ensure that, henceforth, the lnternal Audit
Reports, after perusal by the Deputy Commissioner concerned, are forwarded to the Principals of
the concerned Kendriya Vidyalayas in hard copy, with a copy to KVS (HQ), as per the existing
procedure. Simultaneously, the Finance Officer/concerned officer ofthe Regional Office shall put
up the approved lnternal Audit Report in e-Office and forward it to the Deputy Commissioner
concerned through the e-Office receipt/file process, The Deputy Commissioner shall thereafter
forward the report through e-Office to the undersigned for information and further necessary
action.
It may be ensured that the lnternalAudit Reports are invariably forwarded through e-Office
in addition to the existing procedure of sending hard copies.
