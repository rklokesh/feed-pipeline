Subject: Identity Verification (IV) for the candidates shortlisted in FRTA of
Combined Hindi Translators Examination, 2025 - reg.
The Commission has declared First Round of Tentative Allocation (FRTA) for
Combined Hindi Translators Examination, 2025 vide result writeup dated 05.11.2026.
2. As per the said result writeup, Sliding Process and Identity Verification (IV) for
provisionally shortlisted candidates, will be conducted at the cities of the concerned
Regional Offices on 18.11.2026. Eligible candidates can access and download their
sliding process/ identity verification slip by logging into the designated module
available on the Commission’s official website (https://ssc.gov.in/) starting from
13.11.2026 at 05:00 PM onwards.
3. Candidates must note that physical presence at the designated venue on the
scheduled date is strictly mandatory. Any candidate failing to attend the process in
person will be treated as absent and shall not be considered for the final result. Any
representation regarding change/ allotment of Venue or Date of 'IV', will not be
entertained. The decision of the Commission shall be final and binding.
