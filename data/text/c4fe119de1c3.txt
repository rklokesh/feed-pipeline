7. The offer of appointment to the candidates whose result has been kept
provisional will not be issued till the verification of the original documents
awaited from such candidates. The provisionality of these candidates will
remain valid only for a period of three months from the date of declaration of
Final result. In case the candidate fails to submit the requisite documents
within this period, their candidature will be cancelled and no further
correspondence would be entertained in this regard.
8. The candidates may obtain information/clarification regarding their
issue(s), if any, in relation to the Examination under reference from the
Commission’s Call Centre (Tel. 011-40303444/24041001) from 09:00 AM to
08:00 PM. The result will also be available on the UPSC web site i.e.
www.upsc.gov.in. Marks of the canddiates are likely to be made available on
the website within fifteen days from the date of publication of the result.
