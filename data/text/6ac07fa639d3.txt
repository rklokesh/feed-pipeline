Pay & Allowances:
1. The Visiting Faculty/Adjunct Faculty (Professor) shall be entitled to an honorarium of
₹6,000/- (Rupees Six Thousand only) per day.
2. The Visiting Faculty/Adjunct Faculty (Professor) shall be entitled for reimbursement of
travel expense would be as per extant TA Rules and it would be limited to actual
expenditure incurred subject to production of proof of travel for a maximum of six
outstation journey per year by shortest route.
Scope of Work:
1. Visiting/Adjunct Faculty will be assigned teaching responsibilities. He/she should conduct at
least 4 sessions in a month, such as theory, small group discussions, practical’s,
community based sessions or clinical/bed-side teaching sessions, etc., with each session of
not less than 3 hours’ duration.
2. He/she shall not be involved in the routine administrative work of the department and shall
not replace the roles and responsibilities of the full-time faculty in the department. Six
monthly feedback on teaching by the him/her will be obtained and assessed by this hospital.
