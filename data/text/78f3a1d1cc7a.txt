Necessary information related to e-mail, correspondence
It is humble request to follow the following instructions while
sending any e-mail and letter to RRC/CR.
1. Please e-mail your name, father’s name, C.E.N. No / E.N. No,
applied RRB date of birth, Registration number and specific details.
2. After reading all information available in C.E.N. No / E.N. No and
notices published on the website, e-mail only for actual and
specific informations.
3. Give a brief description of the information you want in a decent
and dignified language. Legal and police action will be taken in
case of indecent language.
4. Hypothetical questions such as, When will the result come, When
will the exams be held etc. not to be e-mailed. General information
is made available on the website. Do not email personal problems
by creating hypothetical situation in this regard.
5. Information about any malpractice noticed by any job seekers can
be brought to the notice of RRC/CR with documentary
proof/evidence.
6. Emails will not be replied to, if any of the above instructions are
violated from above Para 1 to 5 or if they do not belong to RRC/CR.

Public Notice
The only official web-site of Railway Recruitment Cell/Central
Railway is www.rrccr.com.
For any notice/information regarding RRC/CR, the candidate
should visit official web-site of RRC/CR only. Any information/notice
being circulated on social media regarding RRC/CR may please be
ignored.
Railway Recruitment Cell, Central Railway has not appointed any
Agents or Coaching Centres for action on its behalf. Candidates are
warned not to fall under the lure against any such claims being made by
any Persons/Agencies.
"Beware of Touts and job Racketeers trying to deceive by false
promise of securing job in Railways, either through influence or by use
of unfair means". Candidates attempting to use unfair means shall be
disqualified & legal action shall be initiated against them.
