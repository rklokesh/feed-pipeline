Information regarding the city of examination and Admission Certificate for the
candidates of Junior Engineer Examination, 2026 (Computer Based Examination) Paper-I.
As per Notice published by the Commission, Junior Engineer Examination, 2026
(CBE) Paper-I is scheduled to be held from 02.11.2026 to 06.11.2026. The candidates can
view their examination city details by logging in through the designated login module
on the website of the Commission (https://ssc.gov.in/).
2. The ‘Admission Certificate’ for the said examinations will tentatively be available for
download prior to 2-3 days of respective examination date. The same can be accessed
through the designated login module on the website of the commission in a similar
manner. Detailed instructions in this regard are available in the Notice published on
02.09.2026 on the website of the Commission.
3. The candidates availing the ‘Own Scribe’ facility, may require to do fresh Scribe
registration on the Commission’s website, as the registration of scribes prior to
05.11.2025 were disposed of.
4. The Scribe registration has now been integrated with the Aadhaar Authentication.
Therefore, it is mandatory that all such persons, willing to enrol/appear as a scribe, may
complete their fresh registration on the Commission’s website.
5. Candidates who are willing to avail the ‘Own Scribe’ facility in any of these
examinations will be required to submit their Scribe’s registration number for generation
of their Scribe’s Entry Pass. It has been decided by the Commission that the maximum
permissible age limit of persons appearing as own Scribe will be as per the updated
policy. Rest of the guidelines regarding the Scribe facility will remain same as per the
notice of the examination.
6. Candidates who have opted for ‘own scribe’ will be able to map their Scribe OTR
till 27.10.2026 (11:00 PM). Scribe Entry Pass will be generated only for those candidates
who successfully map their Scribe OTR.
7. Candidates must note that the exam will be held for 6 specific listed papers viz. General
Engineering in the disciplines of Civil & Structural, Electrical and Mechanical
Engineering & for IMD Scientific Assistant (SA) in the disciplines of Computer Science and
Information Technology, Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering and Physics from
2nd to 6th November 2026. The exam for post of JE (Telecommunication) will be scheduled
on 30th November 2026 - 1st December 2026 for which the city live will be done 8-10 days
in advance.
8. Further, certain Candidates who had opted for JE(Telecommunication) were also eligible
to apply for JE (Electrical) and Scientific Assistant (SA). For such candidates who have
selected Scientific Assistant (SA), the Commission has decided to consider EC/TC as the
default subject for Scientific Assistant (SA). However, such candidates who wish to change
their subject to CS/IT or Physics will be allowed to do so at the time of City Live till
27.10.2026 (11:00 PM). No further change will be permitted and the candidate has to
appear in the subject opted by him/her which appears in the Admission Certificate.
9. As per practice, Admission Certificates for all the exams will be issued 2/3 days in
advance.
