PUBLIC NOTICE
Dated: 28.10.2026
Subject: Advisory and Instructions regarding registration process for Joint
Entrance Examination (Main) – 2027 (Session 1)
The National Testing Agency will conduct Joint Entrance Examination (Main) – 2027
in two session, Session-1 in January 2027 and Session-2 in April 2027. The online
application form for Joint Entrance Examination (Main) – 2027 (Session 1) is available
on the NTA website https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/.
2. To facilitate a smooth and streamlined registration process for JEE (Main) 2027,
the following clarifications are issued:
(i) Candidates are advised to log in using their own DigiLocker/ JanParichay/e-Pramaan
(MeriPehchaan) credentials to avoid discrepancies in identity verification and biometric
data at subsequent stages of the application process.
(ii) Candidates are advised to do Re-KYC through DigiLocker before filling out the JEE
(Main) 2027 online application form to ensure that their latest details are fetched.
Candidates can click here for the process.
(iii) Candidate’s name and date of birth in the application form must be the same as
mentioned in Class 10th Certificate/ Mark sheet.
3. Candidates are advised to regularly visit the NTA official website for further
updates, instructions, or notices regarding the Joint Entrance Examination – 2027.
