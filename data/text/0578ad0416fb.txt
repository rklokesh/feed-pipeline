PUBLIC NOTICE
Dated: 30.10.2026
Subject: Clarification regarding submission of a fresh Online Application Form for
JEE (Main) – 2027 (Session 1)
In continuation of the Public Notice dated 24.10.2026, NTA has received representations from candidates
regarding errors made while filling out the Online Application Form for JEE (Main) – 2027 (Session 1).
In the interest of candidates, it has been decided to allow them to submit a fresh Application Form for Session
1, even if they have already generated an Application Number or paid the examination fee. The following
conditions apply:
Valid application: Only the last Application Form for which the examination fee has been successfully paid
and the Confirmation Page generated will be considered valid. If a candidate submits a fresh form but does
not complete the fee payment and generate the Confirmation Page, the earlier form for which these steps were
completed will remain valid.
Fresh application: A new Application Number will be generated for each fresh form. All details, including
photograph, signature and documents, must be entered and uploaded again.
Last date: Fresh applications must be completed, including fee payment, on or before 22nd November 2026
upto 11:50 P.M. and with late fee from 23rd November 2026 to 26th November 2026 upto 11:50 P.M.
Fee: The examination fee paid against an earlier form will neither be refunded nor adjusted against the fresh
application. Candidates who only need to amend certain details are advised to use the correction facility,
available from 27th November 2026 to 29th November 2026, instead of submitting a fresh form.
One attempt per session: Submitting multiple Application Forms does not entitle a candidate to appear more
than once in the same session. A candidate who appears, or attempts to appear, in the examination using more
than one Application Number will be treated as having used Unfair Means (UFM), and action will be taken
as per the Information Bulletin. Merely holding more than one Application Number, without appearing using
them, will not be treated as UFM.
