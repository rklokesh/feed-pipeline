7. Whereas, the Board has prescribed a specific period, from 02.09.2026 to

8. Whereas, the Point No. 13 of Notice dated 27.08.2026 categorically provides
that failure to upload the e-dossier within the stipulated period shall result in rejection

conditions governing the recruitment process. All candidates were, therefore,
required to comply with the prescribed timeline.

9; Whereas, the prescribed period for uploading the e-dossier expired on
16.09.2026, and the applicant submitted her email dated 17.09.2026 &
representation seeking a one time opportunity on 18.09.2026 & 22.09.2026, after
expiry of the stipulated period. The applicant's submission that she was provisionally
shortlisted and secured 196.00 marks in the CBT examination has been duly taken
into consideration. However, provisional shortlisting on the basis of examination
marks does not, by itself, confer an indefeasible right to selection or exempt a
candidate for complying with the notified recruitment conditions.

10. Whereas, the Notice dated 27.08.2026 also provide a specific procedure for
cases involving technical glitches in the OARs module. Under this provision, the
candidate was required to report the technical glitch to the DSSSB helpdesk within
48 hours after expiry of the e-dossier submission period, along with supporting
evidence. An additional period of three days could be granted only after the reported
technical glitch and been substantiated and verified by the Board. This limited
provision does not constitute a general extension of the e-dossier submission
deadline. And as per reply submitted by NIC through IT branch that there is no
technical glitch in OARS portal w.e.f. 02.09.2026 to 16.09.206. Further, it has been
