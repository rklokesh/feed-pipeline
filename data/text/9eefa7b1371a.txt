ONLINE Applications are invited from ELIGIBLE Indian citizens for recruitment of following posts in Level 1 & 2
of Pay Matrix 7 CPC against Scouts and Guides Quota for the year 2026-27 in Northern Railway. The posts are
open to all communities and there is no reservation for any communities. Candidates belonging to SC/ST/OBC
categories who will apply against this notification will be extended relaxation in age limit as admissible in a
general open market recruitment.

Applicant can apply for both categories i.e. Level 1 & 2 subject to fulfilling eligibility conditions & given
below in para 1.0. However, candidate need to submit separate application form along with examination
fee for each application. Separate examinationwill be conducted for both the categories, tentatively

on the same day in Two Shifts.

Mere Registration/filling up of online application on RRC website does not entitle the candidate
for Written Examination. Candidature of the applicant is provisional at all stages of selection

process subject to fulfilling the eligibility conditions.

4.0 EXAMINATION FEE:

Examination fee,is to be paid through ONLINE MODE and RRC will not accept Examination fee
in Cash/ Cheque/Moneyorder/IPO/DemandDraft/CentralRecruitment/FeeStampsetc. Candidates
to note that RRC/NR is not responsible for incomplete or pending online examination fee transaction
for whatever reason. In such case, candidate need to proceed with fresh/new transaction for
paymentof examination fee.

(i) For all candidates except those mentioned in sub-para (ii) below: Rs. 500/- (Rupees
Five Hundred) only through Online Payment in favour of RRC/NR. Out of this fee of Rs
500 an amount of Rs 400 shall be refunded in due course duly deducting bank charges
as applicable on appearing in the Written examination/CBT.

(ii) For candidates belonging to SC/ST/Ex-Serviceman/Persons with Benchmark
Disabilities (PWBD), Transgender/Women, Minorities and Economically Backward
Classes Rs. 250/- (Rupees Two Hundred Fifty only) with a provision for refunding the
same (deducting bank charges as applicable) to those who actually appear in the
writter Examination.

(iii) Candidate to note that incase of refund of Examination Fee, it will be transferred back
to the source from where it is received in RRC Account so it in the interest of the
candidate that online fee must be paid using own account.

NOTE:- ‘Minority’ means Muslims, Christians, Buddhists, Zoroastrians (Parsis) & Sikhs.
Economically Backward Classes will mean those candidates whose family
income is less than Rs. 50,000/- per annum.

The following officials are authorized to issue income certificate for the purpose of identifying
Economically Backward Classes:

(a) District Magistrate or any other Revenue Officer up to the level of Tehsildar.

(b) Sitting MP of Lok Sabha for persons of their own constituency.
(c) BPL Card or any other certificate issued by Central Government under a
recognized poverty alleviation programme or Izzat MST issued by Railways.

(0) Union Minister may also recommend for any persons from anywhere in the country.
(e) Sitting MP of Rajya Sabha for person of the district in which the MP normally resides.

The income certificate issued by the authorities mentioned above must be as per format given at
Annexure- ‘E’. This will have to be issued on the letterhead of the issuing authority.

5.0 Scheme for recruitment:

Candidates who applied in response to notification issued and found eligible after scrutiny will be
called for written examination i.e Computer Based Test/Tablet Based Test. The candidates
qualified in written test/Computer Based Test/Tablet Based Test will have to undergo the
Document Verification. Panel will be formed as per merit Position of successful candidates in

both written examination and marks of the certificate. The Candidate will be assessed on the
basis of followin criteria.
