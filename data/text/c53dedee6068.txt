27 October 2026

Subject: Correction in Particulars of the Online Application Form of Rashtriya Military School
Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) 2026 -Reg.

The National Testing Agency is in receipt of representations from the candidates regarding giving them
an opportunity to edit/modify their particulars in the Online Application Form of Rashtriya Military
School Common Entrance Test (RMS CET) — 2026.

In view of the above and the interest of the students, it has been decided to provide an opportunity for the
candidates to correct certain fields/ particulars in the Online Application Form of RMS CET-2026. The
schedule for the same is given below:

All the registered candidates for the said Examination are advised to visit the website
https://exams.nta.nic.in/rmscet/. and verify their particulars. They are further advised to make
correction/s in their particulars, in their respective Application Form, if required.

The candidates are allowed to make the corrections, latest by 28 October 2026 (up to 11:50 P.M.).
Thereafter, no correction in particulars, whatsoever, will be entertained by NTA under any circumstances.
The additional fee (wherever applicable) shall be paid by the candidate concerned either through Credit
Card/Debit Card/Net Banking/UPI.

Since it is a one-time facility extended to the candidates to avoid any hardship, the candidates are advised
to do the correction very carefully, as no further opportunity for correction will be given to the candidates.

The candidates are advised to regularly visit the official NTA website for the latest updates regarding the
examination.
