clearance of case pendency. They are neither sanctioned regular posts nor do they carry any of the pay

4.

with the application, a certificate from the University / Board converting the grade or CGPA into a
percentage of marks. In the absence of such a certificate, the application is liable to be rejected.

4. MODE OF SELECTION

1.

Screening : All applications received within the prescribed date and time shall be screened strictly on the
basis of the essential educational qualification and the requisite post-qualification experience prescribed
at Section 1 above.

Merit for shortlisting : Where the number of eligible applications for a post is disproportionately large, a
post-wise shortlist may be drawn up on the basis of the average of the percentage of marks obtained in
10+2, Bachelor's and Master's examinations. In the case of equal merit, the candidate senior in age shall
be given preference. Merit list will be prepared separately post wise.

Interview : Shortlisted candidates shall be called for interview before a Selection Committee constituted
by the Director, FSL (Haryana), at the Forensic Science Laboratory, Haryana, Madhuban, Karnal. There
shall be no written examination, in the interest of speed.

The list of candidates shortlisted for interview shall be displayed on the Notice Board of the Laboratory

and uploaded on the official website haryanapolice.gov.in. No separate call letter shall be issued by
post. Candidates are advised to check the website regularly.
