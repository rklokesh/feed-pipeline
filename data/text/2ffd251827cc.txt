IMPORTANT NOTICE
COAST GUARD ENROLLED PERSONS TEST (CGEPT)
BATCHES 01/2027 & 02/2027 (CBE)
1. As per Notice published by the Commission, Coast Guard Enrolled Personnel Test
(CGEPT) – Batches 01/2027 & 02/2027 (CBE) is scheduled to be held from 17.11.2026 to
18.11.2026.
2. The Examination City details are available on the examination portal. Candidates
are advised to log in to the portal and check their respective examination city. Further, the
‘Admission Certificate’ for the said examination will tentatively be available for download
prior to 2-3 days of respective examination date.
3. The candidates can view the details by following the procedure mentioned below: -
3.1. Visit the official website of SSC: https://ssc.gov.in/.
3.2. Click on the hyperlink titled “JOIN INDIAN COAST GUARD” available on the
website.
3.3. You will be redirected to the Indian Coast Guard examination portal:
https://cgept.parikshasaarthi.in/.
3.4. Login to the portal using your registered login credentials.
4. You are required to read the instructions given on the E-Admit Card for strict
adherence.
