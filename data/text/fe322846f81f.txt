Subject: Uploading of Final Answer Key(s) along with Candidates’
Response Sheet(s) and Marks of candidates of Combined Higher
Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, 2025 (Tier-II) -regarding.
Staff Selection Commission declared the Final Result of Combined
Higher Secondary Level Examination, 2025 on 07.10.2026.
2. The Commission has uploaded the Final Answer Key(s) along with
Candidates’ Response Sheet(s) of Combined Higher Secondary Level
Examination, 2025 (Tier-II) on the Commission’s website on 17.11.2026.
Candidates may check their individual Final Answer Key(s) along with
Candidates’ Response Sheet(s) from 17.11.2026 (23:00 hrs) to 16.12.2026
(23:00 hrs) by logging in through their Registered ID and Password on the
Login link given below.
3. Further, marks of the qualified/non-qualified candidates have also been
made available on the Commission’s website, i.e. https://ssc.gov.in, from
17.11.2026 (23:00 hrs) to 16.12.2026 (23:00 hrs), which can be accessed
by the candidates by logging in through their Registered ID and Password on
the website of the Commission.
4. The candidates may take a printout of their respective Final Answer
Key(s) along with Candidates’ Response Sheet(s) as well as scorecard/marks,
as the same will not be made available after the above-specified time
limit.
5. Candidates may note that the saved Question Paper is strictly for their
personal use and self-analysis only.
