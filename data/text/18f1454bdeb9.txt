which is a test to determine the eligibility of Indian nationals for ‘award of Junior Research Fellowship and
appointment as Assistant Professor’, 'appointment as Assistant Professor and admission to Ph.D.' and ‘admission
to Ph.D. only’ in Indian universities and colleges through Computer Based Test (CBT) mode.

In accordance with the Information Bulletin and Public Notice, candidates are being provided an opportunity to
correct particulars in their Online Application Forms for Joint CSIR-UGC NET December 2026 as per the
schedule below:
