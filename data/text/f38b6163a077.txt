OFFICE ORDER
Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan vide office-order of even number dated 03.10.2025, conveyed
the approval of Government of India, for establishing 57 new Kendriya Vidyalayas under
Civil/Defence Sector with the stipulation that the sponsoring authority concerned is required to
transfer the identified demarcated land and also to give possession of the temporary accommodation
to Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan prior to opening of the new Kendriya Vidyalaya. Kendriya
Vidyalaya Biharsharif City, District Nalanda, Bihar in Parliamentary Constituency Nalanda is
one of the S7 new Kendriya Vidyalayas sanctioned in October 2025.
Since the land in the matter of this Kendriya Vidyalaya has been transferred by the
Sponsoring Authority in favour of Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan and temporary accommodation
has been found suitable, sanction is hereby conveyed to start a new Kendriya Vidyalaya under Civil
Sector with effect from the session 2027-28 at the following location:
KV Name of Kendriya Vidyalaya Kendriya Vidyalaya will be made functional during
Code Academic Year 2027-28 at:
2570 Kendriya Vidyalaya Kendriya Vidyalaya Biharsharif City, School Building,
Biharsharif City, District Nalanda University, Rajgir, Chhabilapur Road, Rajgir,
Nalanda, Bihar District Nalanda, Bihar - 803116
The above Vidyalaya will start functioning from Balvatika I to III, class I to V (single section
in each class) and thereafter will grow consequently based on feasibility as per approval.
The admission process may be completed according to the schedule of admission prescribed
for the academic year 2027-28 after completion of all pending formalities of transfer of land and
temporary accommodation, if any.
