Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD)
in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026– Shortlisting of candidates to appear in Physical
Efficiency Test (PET)/Physical Standard Test (PST).
Staff Selection Commission conducted Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces
(CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026 from 27.04.2026
to 30.05.2026 in the Computer Based Mode. A total of 21,56,776 candidates appeared in the
Computer Based Examination.

3. As per Para 14.2 of the Notice of the Examination, the following minimum qualifying
marks (without adding bonus marks to NCC certificate holders) have been applied to
consider eligibility for shortlisting to the next stage of the Examination i.e., PET/PST:-
i. UR: 30%
ii. OBC/ EWS: 25%
iii. All other categories (SC, ST, ESM): 20%
4. As per para 14.18 of the Notice of Examination, candidates belonging to
naxal/militancy affected areas have been first considered against the vacancies of
naxal/militancy affected areas and then against the vacancies of general areas. Similarly,
candidates belonging to border areas have been first considered against the vacancies of
border area and then against the vacancies of general area. Candidates belonging to both
areas i.e. naxal/militancy affected area as well as border area have been first considered
against the vacancies of naxal/militancy affected area followed by the vacancies of border
area and general area respectively.
5. As per Para 12.1.5 of the Notice of Examination, marks scored by candidates in the
Computer Based Examination have been normalized by using the procedure published by the
Commission vide Notice dated 02.06.2025 and such normalized scores along with the bonus
marks for NCC Certificate holders have been used to determine the cut-off marks for
shortlisting of candidates for PET/PST.

9. As per the option exercised by the candidates in their Online Application Form,
candidates belonging to SC, ST, and OBC categories on migration from one State / UT (i.e.,
Origin) to another State / UT (i.e., Domiciled) have been considered in their respective
reserved category if they have opted to take benefit of reservation from the State / UT of their
Origin. Otherwise, as per the option exercised by such candidates in their Online Application
Form, they have been considered as UR candidates from the State / UT of their domicile as
per Para 6.3 of the Notice of the Examination. Accordingly, the result of 13231 candidates
whose category has been changed to UR based on their option in their Online Application
Form has been processed under the UR category. Out of these 13231 candidates, 246 have
been provisionally shortlisted to appear in PET/PST. These 246 candidates have been
included in List I or List II, as the case may be. Age relaxation codes of these candidates
have also been modified accordingly.
10. Border Guarding District and Militancy/ Naxal Affected District status of the
candidates has been considered as per the list of districts given at Annexure-XI and
Annexure-XII of the Notice of Examination.
11. RESOLUTION OF TIE CASES
In accordance with Para 15 of the Notice of Examination, in the event of tie in scores
of candidates in the Computer Based Examination (including NCC bonus marks), such cases
have been resolved by applying the following criteria, one after another in the given order, till
the tie is resolved:
a. Marks in Part-A of the Computer-Based Examination (General Intelligence and
Reasoning).
b. Marks in Part-B of the Computer-Based Examination (General Knowledge and
General Awareness).
c. Marks in Part-C of the Computer-Based Examination (Elementary Mathematics).
d. Date of Birth, with older candidates placed higher.
e. Alphabetical order of names of the candidates.

15. In cases where the vacancies are available, but the candidates are not available (cut-
off not available in Annexure–I & II of the result write-up), these vacancies may get filled
up in the subsequent stage(s) of the Examination. This is due to the fact that unfilled
vacancies in the reserved category may occur on account of candidates belonging to these
categories qualifying at the UR cut-off in CBE. At later stages, these candidates may qualify
in their respective vertical categories. The same principle is also applicable for General Area /
Border Area/ Militancy / Naxal Affected Area vacancies within a State / UT.
15.1. For all such vacancies where candidates are not available, i.e. no candidates have
been shortlisted at this stage, minimum qualifying marks as applicable to respective
categories in terms of Para 14.2 of the Notice of the Examination has been taken as
cut-off marks for subsequent stages. Also, for all such vacancies where less than
required number of candidates has been shortlisted, minimum qualifying marks as
applicable to respective categories has been taken as cut-off marks to shortlist for
PET/PST.

information.
