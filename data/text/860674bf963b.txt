DETAILED ADVERTISEMENT FOR RECRUITMENT OF MERITORIOUS
SPORTSPERSONS TO THE POST OF CONSTABLE (GENERAL DUTY)
UNDER SPORTS QUOTA-2025 IN ITBPF.
Online Applications are invited from eligible Male and Female Indian
citizens (including subject of Nepal & Bhutan) possessing educational qualification
& age as prescribed below for filling up following vacancies for the post of
Constable (General Duty) under Sports Quota in Group ‘C’ (Non-Gazetted,
Non-Ministerial) on temporary basis, likely to be permanent in ITBPF against
Sports Quota. Selected candidates will be liable to serve anywhere in India or
abroad. On appointment, the candidates shall be governed by the ITBPF Act 1992
and Rules 1994 and other Rules applicable from time to time. Applications from
candidates will be accepted through online mode only. No other mode for
submission of application is allowed. Online application mode will be opened
w.e.f. 3rd October, 2026 (03/10/2026) at 00:01 A.M and will be closed on 01st
November 2026 (01/11/2026) at 11:59 P.M. The applicants are advised to check
their eligibility before applying so as to avoid disappointment at a later stage.

Note :-
a. The recruitment is only for meritorious sportsperson who are either medal
winners/position holders and/or participants as per Para-4(c) below.
b. The vacancies are tentative and may increase or decrease without any
notice. Any change in the number of vacancies will be intimated through
ITBPF Recruitment website i.e. https://recruitment.itbpolice.nic.in.
c. ITBPF reserves the right to make changes in sequence of the recruitment
procedure after publication of this advertisement. ITBPF also reserves the
right to cancel or postpone the recruitment at any stage due to administrative
reasons.
d. In case, suitable candidates are not found in any one or more of the
disciplines/sport events advertised, the ITBPF reserves the right to transfer
the vacancies reserved for such disciplines, categories to any of the
remaining sports disciplines/sport events in the aforementioned list.
