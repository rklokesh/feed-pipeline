3. Pay and Allowances: Fixed Basic- ₹ 78,800/- & other allowance. Tax and other deductions will be as per prevailing

4. Terms of Contract:
(i) The tenure of all above post(s) will be for 01 (One) year OR attainment of 70 years of age; whichever is earlier
from the date of joining with provision of yearly extension up to another 02 (Two) years on same terms &
conditions and pay on satisfactory performance, willingness and conduct of candidate and requirement of this
Institution. Selected candidate has to submit application for extension every year.
(ii) This, being a contractual appointment, absence from work for a period beyond the 07 (Seven) days without
proper permission of the competent authority will amount to voluntary abandonment of engagement and
automatic termination. The teaching faculty is required to mark attendance manually as well as on Aadhaar
Enabled Bio-metric Attendance System (AEBAS) daily during scheduled working days. Monthly Pay will be paid
as per AEBAS attendance marked by the person concerned for his/ her working hours.
(iii) The contractual engagement may be terminated/ discontinued from either side giving one-month prior notice
to this effect without assigning reason. In case of spot resignation by the selected candidate, one month’s
salary is to be deposited to ESIC Account through Challan.
(iv) No private practice is allowed in any capacity after joining as teaching faculty at ESIC Medical College, Bihta.
(v) No claim for any service benefits like PF, Pension, Gratuity, Medical Allowance/ Benefits, Seniority, Promotion
etc. will be admissible.
(vi) Acquired/ Accumulated Leave for 2.5 (Two and a Half) days may be availed for each completed month of work
with pre approval of the Competent Authority.
(vii) The selected candidate must have a Professional Indemnity Policy of Rs. 30,00,000/- (Thirty Lakhs) only which
should cover the period as desired by ESIC. The policy is meant to cover the profession liability falling on them
as a result of errors and omissions committed by them while rendering professional services.
(viii) Other terms and condition will be applicable as issued by Competent Authority from time to time.
