About this notice

The Staff Selection Commission has issued a corrigendum notice dated 26 October 2026 regarding the revision of examination dates for two major recruitments: Junior Engineer Examination, 2026 (Paper-I) and Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination, 2026. As per the notice, the Junior Engineer Paper-I will now be conducted from 02nd to 06th November, 2026 and 30th November to 01st December, 2026. The Sub-Inspector examination will be held from 23rd to 28th November, 2026. Candidates are advised to check the official website regularly for further updates.
