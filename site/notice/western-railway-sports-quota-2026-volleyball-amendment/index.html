About this notice

Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC) Western Railway has published an amendment to Employment Notification No. RRC/WR/01/2026 for Sports Quota recruitment. This notice specifically updates the criteria and eligibility for sports achievements and certificates in the volleyball discipline. Candidates belonging to the volleyball game discipline must carefully check the revised norms regarding federation certificates and valid recognition periods before applying for the recruitment.

What changed
Para 3.4.8 (Volleyball Sports Achievements)Previous volleyball achievement validity rules as per original notification → Revised validity rules including specific recognition window from 29.10.2025 to 21.04.2026 for VFI National Championships

  • Sports persons applying for RRC Western Railway Sports Quota recruitment under the volleyball discipline

  • Candidates whose volleyball certificates fall under restricted periods without valid government recognition

Frequently asked questions

What is the advertisement number for RRC Western Railway Sports Quota Recruitment 2026?

The advertisement number is RRC/WR/01/2026(Sports Quota).

Which sport discipline does this amendment affect?

This amendment affects candidates applying under the Volleyball game discipline.

Are certificates from the Volleyball Federation of India issued after 02/01/2017 valid?

Sports achievements in events conducted by the Volleyball Federation of India or its affiliated units after 02/01/2017 till the notice date will generally not be considered, except for National Championships during the interim body recognition period from 29.10.2025 to 21.04.2026.

Which period of VFI National Championships is considered valid?

National Volleyball Championships conducted under the aegis of VFI during the interim body recognition period from 29.10.2025 to 21.04.2026 are considered valid.

Are Association of Indian Universities certificates accepted for volleyball?

Yes, certificates issued by the Association of Indian Universities and International Federations for recognized sports achievements in volleyball are permissible.

When was this amendment notice issued?

The amendment notice was issued on 03 September 2026.

Where can I view the official amendment notice?

You can view the official notice on the RRC Western Railway official website at www.rrc-wr.com.
