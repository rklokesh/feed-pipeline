About this notice

The Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission (CGPSC) has announced the written examination result for the State Engineering Service Exam 2026. The written exam was conducted on 16 June 2026. A total of 142 candidates have been provisionally selected for the next stage based on their performance. Candidates can view their roll numbers, names, and date of birth in the merit list available on the official website.

How to download

  1. Visit the official CGPSC website at www.psc.cg.gov.in.
  2. Click on the Result section.
  3. Open the State Engineering Service Exam 2026 written exam result PDF.
  4. Check your roll number and name in the list.

Good to know

What is CGPSC State Engineering Service Exam?

It is a state-level competitive examination conducted by the Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission to recruit eligible candidates for engineering posts in various departments.
