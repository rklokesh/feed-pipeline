About this notice

The Office of the Joint Director, School Education, Jaipur Division has released the instructions and schedule for the document verification of provisionally selected candidates for the Fourth Class Employee Direct Recruitment 2024. Candidates who are in the merit list must appear in person at the office with two sets of detailed scrutiny forms and self-attested copies of all required original documents. All candidates should carefully read the notification and guidelines before attending the verification process.

How to download
  1. Download the Detailed Form Cum Scrutiny Form from the RSSB website if not already filled online.
  2. Fill out the two sets of scrutiny forms and attach self-attested photocopies of all required educational, category, and residential documents in the specified order.
  3. Attach two passport-size photographs, a self-attested copy of e-Aadhaar with QR code, and valid photo ID.
  4. Present yourself in person at the designated office on the scheduled date and time with all original documents and two sets of files.
Documents to keep ready
  • Detailed Form Cum Scrutiny Form (Two sets)
  • Secondary mark sheet and certificate for date of birth verification
  • Complete educational mark sheets and certificates
  • Bonafide resident certificate (Special TSP resident certificate if applicable)
  • Category certificate (SC, ST, OBC, MBC, EWS, Sahariya) issued by the last date of online application
  • Handwritten sample sheet in Hindi and English
  • Two passport-size photographs and self-attested photo ID (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Driving Licence, Passport)
  • E-Aadhaar card containing QR code
  • Character certificates (Two from different Gazetted Officers and one from the last attended educational institution)
  • Marriage certificate and affidavit regarding dowry/no-dowry and children (if applicable)
  • Widow/Divorced documents and self-declarations (if applicable)
  • Disability certificate from a competent medical board (if applicable)
  • Sports certificates from competent authorities (if applicable)
  • Ex-servicemen discharge book/noc/self-declaration (if applicable)
  • Notarized affidavit for self-verification of documents and non-involvement in criminal cases
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Failing to bring two complete sets of documents arranged in the specified order with page numbers
  • Submitting category certificates issued after the last date of online application without the required affidavit
  • Mismatch between photographs or signatures uploaded in the online form and those presented during verification
  • Failing to submit the handwritten sample matching the prescribed format
Good to know

What is RSSB Fourth Class Employee Document Verification?

It is the final verification stage where provisionally selected candidates must present their original educational, category, and identity documents to prove their eligibility for appointment.

What happens next
  1. Final appointment orders will be issued by the competent authority after successful completion and verification of documents.

Frequently asked questions

What documents are required for RSSB Fourth Class Employee Document Verification?

Candidates must bring two sets of detailed scrutiny forms, educational mark sheets, date of birth proof, caste certificate, bonafide resident certificate, character certificates, ID proof, and self-attested photocopies of all supporting documents.

What should I do if I have not filled the online Detailed Scrutiny Form?

If you have not filled it online due to unavoidable reasons, you can download the form from the RSSB website, fill it manually, and submit it along with a ₹100 postal order in favor of the Secretary, RSSB Jaipur, explaining the reason.

How many sets of documents need to be submitted?

Candidates must prepare and submit two complete sets of documents along with the detailed scrutiny forms.

Is a caste certificate issued after the last date of the online application valid?

Caste certificates should ideally be issued by the last date of the online application. If issued later, an affidavit must be submitted.

Final checklist
  • Download and fill two sets of the Detailed Scrutiny Form.
  • Arrange all original certificates along with self-attested photocopies in two separate files.
  • Attach required affidavits, character certificates, and handwritten samples.
  • Carry valid photo ID, e-Aadhaar with QR code, and passport-size photographs to the venue on time.
