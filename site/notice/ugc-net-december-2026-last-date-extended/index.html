About this notice

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has issued a public notice regarding the extension of the last date for submission of online application forms for the UGC-NET December 2026 Examination. Candidates who have not yet completed their application can now submit their forms and fees by 31 October 2026 up to 11:50 PM. This examination determines the eligibility of Indian nationals for Junior Research Fellowship, Assistant Professor appointment, and Ph.D. admissions in Indian universities and colleges. All other terms, conditions, and information bulletin guidelines remain unchanged.
