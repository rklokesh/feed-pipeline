About this notice

India Post, Mail Motor Services (MMS) Kolkata has released a notification for the direct recruitment of Skilled Artisan (Tradesmen) posts for the West Bengal Circle, totaling 19 vacancies. This notification acts as a corrigendum to previous notices. Eligible and interested candidates can send their offline applications in the prescribed format through Speed Post only by 30 November 2026 up to 17:00 hours. The selection will be based on a competitive trade test. Candidates should ensure they meet all educational, age, and trade requirements before applying.

Who should read this
  • Candidates possessing ITI certificate or 8th pass with 1 year experience in the respective trade
  • Candidates aged between 18 and 30 years meeting the criteria
Who cannot apply
  • Applications sent by courier or any transmission other than speed post
  • Unsigned applications or those without self-attested documents
  • Candidates without valid community or required certificates

Eligibility
Educational QualificationA certificate in the respective trade from any Technical Institution recognized by Government OR VIII Std. passed with experience of one year in the respective trade.
Additional requirement for MV MechanicMust possess a valid Driving Licence (HMV) to drive any vehicle in service in order to test it.
Salary and pay level
Pay ScaleLevel 2 in the pay matrix as per 7th CPC (₹19,900 to ₹63,200) + allowances
Selection process
  1. Scrutiny of offline applications
  2. Competitive Trade Test (syllabus and details informed with hall permits)
How to apply
  1. Download the application form and notification from the official website.
  2. Fill out the application form on plain paper in the prescribed format.
  3. Attach self-attested photocopies of all required certificates and a recent passport-size photograph.
  4. If applying for multiple trades, submit separate applications for each trade.
  5. Superscribe the envelope with 'Application for the post of /Name of Trade: ...' and send it through Speed Post only to 'The Senior Manager, Mail Motor Services, 139, Beleghata Road, Kolkata-700015'.
Documents to keep ready
  • Age proof (Birth certificate/Class 10th certificate/School leaving certificate)
  • Address proof
  • Indian Citizenship proof (Voter ID, Passport, Domicile, etc.)
  • Educational qualification certificates
  • Technical qualification certificates (ITI/etc.)
  • HMV Driving Licence/Licence Extract (for MV Mechanic only)
  • One year trade experience certificate on firm's letterhead with full postal address
  • SC/ST/OBC(NCL)/ESM Certificate in proper format
  • Two self-attested passport-size photographs
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Do not send applications through courier or normal post; use Speed Post only.
  • Do not forget to self-attest all attached documents.
  • Do not send original certificates, only send self-attested photocopies.
  • Ensure separate applications are sent for each trade if applying for more than one.
Good to know

What is Skilled Artisan Recruitment in India Post MMS Kolkata?

It is a direct recruitment process to fill vacant artisan and tradesmen positions such as MV Mechanic, Electrician, Tyreman, Blacksmith, Painter, and Carpenter under Mail Motor Services, Kolkata in the West Bengal Circle.

What happens next
  1. Candidates shortlisted after application scrutiny will be called for a competitive trade test.
  2. Final selection will lead to appointment on probation for one year.
Other details
Probation periodOne year
Previous applicantsAll previous applications in response to previous notifications (dated 08/08/2018 and 19.05.2020) will be considered, but fresh applications mentioning choices are preferable.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to apply for India Post MMS Kolkata Skilled Artisan Recruitment 2026?

The last date to receive applications is 30 November 2026 up to 17:00 hours.

How many total vacancies are available?

There are a total of 19 vacancies available across various trades.
