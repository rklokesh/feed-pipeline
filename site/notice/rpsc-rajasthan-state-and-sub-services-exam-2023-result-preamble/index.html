Revised result preamble released

Rajasthan Public Service Commission has released a revised result preamble for the Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services Combined Competitive Examination 2023 following court orders. About this notice

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has issued a result preamble for the Rajasthan State and Subordinate Services Combined Competitive Examination 2023. This notice is published in compliance with the order passed by the Hon'ble Rajasthan High Court, Jaipur in S.B. Civil Writ Petition No. 9253/2025. Candidates concerned with the examination results can check the updated merit details mentioned in the official notice.
