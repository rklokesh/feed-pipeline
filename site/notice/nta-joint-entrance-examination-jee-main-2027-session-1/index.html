About this notice

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has released the notification for the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main 2027 Session 1. The exam is conducted for admission to Undergraduate Engineering Programs (B.E/B.Tech), B. Arch, and B. Planning courses at NITs, IIITs, CFTIs, and other participating institutions. Online applications are open from 24th October 2026 to 22nd November 2026. Candidates wishing to seek admission or qualify for JEE (Advanced) should read the notice and apply within the stipulated timeline.

Who should read this
  • Candidates aspiring for admission to engineering, architecture, and planning courses in top technical institutions
Who cannot apply
  • Candidates submitting more than one application form will be disqualified

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to apply for JEE Main 2027 Session 1?

The last date to apply for JEE Main 2027 Session 1 is 22nd November 2026.

What is the application fee for JEE Main 2027?

The application fee is ₹500.

When will the JEE Main 2027 Session 1 exam be conducted?

The exam will be conducted on 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, and 28 January 2027.

What is the official website to apply for JEE Main 2027?

The official website is https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/

When will the admit cards be available for download?

Admit cards will be available for download from 13th January 2027.

Can I apply for Session 2 right now?

No, candidates can apply only for Session 1 currently; a separate opportunity will be given for Session 2 later.

When is the exam city likely to be announced?

The exam city will be announced on 30th December 2026.

What are the papers included in JEE Main?

JEE Main comprises Paper 1 for B.E./B.Tech, Paper 2A for B.Arch, and Paper 2B for B.Planning.
