About this notice

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has released the official notification for the Common Recruitment Process for Specialist Officers (CRP SPL-XVI) for vacancies of the year 2027-28. Eligible candidates can apply online from 01 July 2026 to 21 July 2026. The recruitment process includes an Online Preliminary Examination, Online Main Examination, and a Common Interview. Prospective candidates should carefully read the notification and ensure they meet all eligibility criteria before applying.

Who should read this
  • Graduates and Postgraduates meeting specific professional qualifications looking for Specialist Officer jobs in public sector banks.
Who cannot apply
  • Candidates who do not meet the age limits or educational qualifications specified for the respective posts.

How to apply
  1. Visit the official IBPS website at www.ibps.in.
  2. Click on the Home page to open the link 'Click here to apply for CRP SPL XVI'.
  3. Choose 'Click Here for New Registration' and enter basic details.
  4. Upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  5. Fill out the application form carefully and complete the online fee payment.
Documents to keep ready

  • Mark sheets and degree certificates in support of educational qualifications
  • Caste/Category certificate if applicable
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Failing to check eligibility criteria before applying.
  • Submitting incorrect category details as category cannot be changed after the edit window.
Good to know

What is IBPS CRP SPL-XVI?

It is a Common Recruitment Process conducted annually by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) to select Specialist Officers for participating public sector banks.

What happens next
  1. Candidates qualifying in the Online Preliminary Examination will be called for the Online Main Examination. Shortlisted candidates from the Main exam will proceed to the Common Interview, followed by provisional allotment to participating banks.
