About this notice

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has released the notification for the Common Recruitment Process for Specialist Officers (CRP SPL-XVI) for vacancies of 2027-28. Eligible candidates can register online from 01 July 2026 to 21 July 2026. The recruitment process involves Preliminary examination, Main examination, and Interview for various Scale I Specialist Officer posts in participating banks.

How to apply
  1. Visit the official website of IBPS at www.ibps.in.
  2. Click on the Home Page to open the link 'CRP Specialist Officers'.
  3. Click option 'CLICK HERE TO APPLY ONLINE FOR CRP-Specialist Officers (CRP SPL-XVI)' to open the On-Line Application Form.
  4. Register by entering basic details and upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and handwritten declaration.
  5. Proceed to pay the application fee online and submit the application.
