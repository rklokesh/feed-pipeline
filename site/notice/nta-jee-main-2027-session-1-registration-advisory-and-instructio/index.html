About this notice

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has released an advisory and instructions regarding the online registration process for the Joint Entrance Examination (Main) – 2027 Session 1. NTA will conduct the JEE Main 2027 exam in two sessions: Session 1 in January 2027 and Session 2 in April 2027. Candidates planning to apply online are advised to follow specific guidelines regarding DigiLocker login, Re-KYC, and matching details with their Class 10 certificates. Read this notice carefully before filling out the application form.

Selection process
  1. Online Application with DigiLocker/JanParichay/e-Pramaan login
  2. Computer Based Test (CBT) for JEE Main 2027
How to apply
  1. Visit the official website at https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/
  2. Perform Re-KYC through DigiLocker before filling out the form to fetch latest details
  3. Log in using your own DigiLocker, JanParichay, or e-Pramaan credentials
  4. Ensure candidate's name and date of birth match the Class 10th certificate
Documents to keep ready
  • Class 10th Certificate or Mark sheet
  • DigiLocker / JanParichay / e-Pramaan credentials
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Do not use someone else's DigiLocker or login credentials
  • Do not enter name or date of birth differently from the Class 10th certificate

Frequently asked questions

When will JEE Main 2027 Session 1 be conducted?

JEE Main 2027 Session 1 will be conducted in January 2027 by NTA.

Where can I fill out the JEE Main 2027 application form?

You can fill out the online application form on the official NTA website at https://jeemain.nta.nic.in/.

What login credentials are required for the application process?

Candidates are advised to log in using their own DigiLocker, JanParichay, or e-Pramaan credentials.

Why is DigiLocker Re-KYC recommended before filling the form?

Re-KYC through DigiLocker ensures that your latest details are fetched correctly and avoids discrepancies in identity verification.

What documents must match the candidate's name and date of birth?

The candidate's name and date of birth in the application form must be the same as mentioned in the Class 10th Certificate or Mark sheet.

How many sessions will JEE Main 2027 have?

JEE Main 2027 will be conducted in two sessions: Session 1 in January 2027 and Session 2 in April 2027.

Final checklist
  • Keep your DigiLocker account active and updated
  • Complete Re-KYC in DigiLocker before starting application
  • Keep Class 10 certificate ready for name and DOB verification
