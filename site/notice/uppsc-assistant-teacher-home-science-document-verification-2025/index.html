About this notice

The Uttar Pradesh Public Service Commission (UPPSC) has released the document verification notice for the Assistant Teacher Trained Graduate Grade (Male/Female Branch) Exam 2025 for Home Science. Candidates who qualified must check their document verification schedule and complete the online authentication process on the official website. The verification is scheduled to be held on 29, 30, and 31 October 2026 in multiple shifts. Qualified candidates are advised to download their complete form set and carry all required documents.

How to download

  1. Visit the official website uppsc.up.nic.in
  2. Click on 'Click here for Advt. No. A-5/E-1/2025, Assistant Teacher... Document Verification'
  3. Click on 'Click here to Authenticate' and enter OTR Number
  4. Go to 'Applicant Dashboard' after authentication and check latest status
  5. Click on 'Proceed to Print Complete Form Set for Document Verification', pay the required processing fee, and print the forms along with detailed scrutiny form

Documents to keep ready

  • Complete form set for document verification with detailed scrutiny form
  • Educational qualification certificates
  • Identity proof
