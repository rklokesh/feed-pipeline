About this notice

The Staff Selection Commission has announced the shortlisting results for the Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026. The computer-based examination was conducted from 27.04.2026 to 30.05.2026, in which 21,56,776 candidates appeared. Qualified candidates are now eligible to appear in the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST).

How to download
  1. Visit the official website of the Staff Selection Commission (ssc.gov.in).
  2. Check the result notice and look into List-I (Female) and List-II (Male) for shortlisted candidate details.
  3. Visit the CRPF website (rect.crpf.gov.in) for PET/PST call letters and further schedule.

Frequently asked questions

When was the SSC Constable GD CBE exam conducted?

The Computer Based Examination for Constable GD 2026 was conducted from 27.04.2026 to 30.05.2026.

Who will conduct the PET/PST for SSC Constable GD 2026?

The PET/PST will be conducted by the CAPFs, and the Nodal CAPF (CRPF) will issue the call letters.

Where can I download the PET/PST admit card?

Candidates can visit the official CRPF website at https://rect.crpf.gov.in to download their call letters.
