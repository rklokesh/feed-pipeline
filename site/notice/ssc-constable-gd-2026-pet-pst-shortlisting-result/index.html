About this notice

The Staff Selection Commission has announced the results of the Computer Based Examination for Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026. The computer-based test was conducted from 27 April 2026 to 30 May 2026. A total of 21,56,776 candidates appeared in the examination. Candidates who have qualified have been provisionally shortlisted to appear in the next stage comprising the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST). Qualified candidates should check the lists published by the Commission and follow the CRPF website for further schedule.

Who should read this
  • Candidates who appeared in the SSC Constable GD Computer Based Examination 2026.
Who cannot apply
  • Candidates who failed to secure the minimum qualifying marks or cut-off marks.
  • Candidates whose results are withheld or debarred by the Commission.

How to download
  1. Visit the official website of the Staff Selection Commission at https://ssc.gov.in.
  2. Go to the Notice Board or Result section for the Constable GD Examination 2026 shortlisting result.
  3. Check your roll number in the respective lists (List-I for female, List-II for male).
  4. Visit the nodal CRPF website at https://rect.crpf.gov.in for PET/PST call letters and updates.

Good to know

What is SSC Constable GD PET/PST Shortlisting Result?

This is the result of the computer-based written examination for Constable GD and Rifleman GD, through which candidates are shortlisted to appear in the Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test.

What happens next
  1. Shortlisted candidates will appear for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) conducted by CRPF.
  2. Qualified candidates in physical tests will proceed to Detailed Medical Examination and Document Verification.

Frequently asked questions

When was the SSC Constable GD 2026 Computer Based Examination conducted?

The examination was conducted from 27 April 2026 to 30 May 2026.

How many candidates have been shortlisted for PET/PST?

A total of 2,15,677 candidates have been provisionally shortlisted for the Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test.

Where can I download the PET/PST admit card?

Call letters and admit cards for the PET/PST will be issued by CRPF on their official website at https://rect.crpf.gov.in.

What are the minimum qualifying marks for UR category in the CBE?

The minimum qualifying mark for the UR category is 30%.

What are the minimum qualifying marks for OBC and EWS categories?

The minimum qualifying marks for OBC and EWS categories are 25%.

Are NCC bonus marks included in the cut-off calculation?

Yes, normalized scores along with bonus marks for NCC certificate holders have been used to determine the cut-off marks.

Who is conducting the PET/PST stage of the recruitment?

The PET/PST will be conducted by the Central Armed Police Forces, with CRPF acting as the nodal agency.

What is the official website of SSC to check results?

The official website of the Staff Selection Commission is https://ssc.gov.in.

Final checklist
