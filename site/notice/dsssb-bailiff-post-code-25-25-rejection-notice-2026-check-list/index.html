About this notice

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has issued Rejection Notice No. 1666 on 22 October 2026 for the Bailiff post under Post Code 25/25 in Registrar Cooperative Societies. Candidates who were shortlisted for the post were given an opportunity from 20 July 2026 to 03 August 2026 to upload their documents via the e-dossier link on the OARS module. As the listed candidates failed to submit the e-dossiers within the stipulated period, their candidature has been officially rejected by the board.

Who should read this

  • Candidates who appeared for the DSSSB Bailiff Post Code 25/25 Tier-I exam

Who cannot apply
