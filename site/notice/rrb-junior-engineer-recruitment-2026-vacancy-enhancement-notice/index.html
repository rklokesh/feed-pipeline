About this notice

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB) have released a corrigendum notice regarding the enhancement of vacancies for Junior Engineer (P.Way & Signal) posts under Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) 04/2026. This notice updates the earlier published vacancy counts. Candidates who applied for these posts should review the revised allocations.
