About this notice

Railway Recruitment Cell, Western Railway (RRC WR) has released an employment notification for recruitment against Sports Quota for the year 2026-27. A total of 64 vacancies are available for Group C and Group D posts across various sports disciplines. Eligible sportsperson candidates can submit their online applications from 01 September 2026 to 30 September 2026. There is no reservation for SC, ST, OBC, EWS, PwBD, or Ex-SM categories in this recruitment.

Who should read this
  • Active sportsperson candidates meeting the age and educational criteria for RRC Western Railway Sports Quota 2026
Who cannot apply
  • Candidates awaiting final results of the prescribed educational qualification are not eligible to apply
