Updated 30 Oct 2026 · Source: nta.ac.in

Fresh application window open till 22 Nov 2026

NTA has issued a clarification allowing candidates who made errors in their JEE Main 2027 Session 1 form to submit a fresh online application by 22nd November 2026. A fresh application number will be generated, and all details must be re-entered.

About this notice

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has released an important public notice regarding the submission of a fresh Online Application Form for JEE (Main) – 2027 (Session 1). Candidates who have made mistakes in their previously submitted forms are allowed to submit a new application. The last date to submit a fresh application is 22nd November 2026, and with a late fee until 26th November 2026. Candidates should note that the fee paid for earlier forms will neither be refunded nor adjusted.

Who should read this
  • Engineering aspirants who made errors in their JEE Main 2027 Session 1 application form.
Who cannot apply
  • Candidates submitting multiple forms without valid reasons to appear more than once in violation of UFM rules.

Selection process

How to apply
  1. Visit the official website at jeemain.nta.nic.in.
  2. Log in and fill out a fresh application form if you made major errors.
  3. Upload new photograph, signature, and documents.
  4. Complete the fee payment and generate the Confirmation Page.
Documents to keep ready
  • Photograph
  • Signature
  • Required educational and personal documents
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Do not assume earlier fees will be adjusted for the fresh application.
  • Ensure the new application has fee payment completed and confirmation page generated, as incomplete fresh forms will revert validity to the older form.
Good to know

What is the JEE Main 2027 Session 1 fresh application notice?

It is an official clarification by NTA allowing candidates who made errors in their initial application form to submit a completely fresh application for JEE Main 2027 Session 1.

What happens next
  1. Correction window will open from 27th November to 29th November 2026 for minor edits.
  2. Admit card and exam date announcements will follow.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to submit a fresh application for JEE Main 2027 Session 1?

The last date to submit a fresh application is 22nd November 2026 up to 11:50 P.M.

Can I submit a fresh application with a late fee?

Yes, fresh applications with a late fee can be submitted from 23rd November 2026 to 26th November 2026 up to 11:50 P.M.

Will my previous fee be refunded or adjusted?

No, the examination fee paid against an earlier form will neither be refunded nor adjusted against the fresh application.

When is the correction facility available?

The correction facility is available from 27th November 2026 to 29th November 2026.

What happens if I submit a fresh form but do not pay the fee?

If you do not complete the fee payment and generate the confirmation page for the fresh form, your earlier form will remain valid.

Final checklist
  • Check eligibility and details carefully before submitting.
  • Keep scanned copies of photo and signature ready.
  • Ensure fee payment is successfully done.
