About this notice

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has issued the notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP-PO/MT-XVI for recruitment of Probationary Officers and Management Trainees in Participating Public Sector Banks for the vacancies of 2027-28. Online registration starts on 01 July 2026 and closes on 21 July 2026. Eligible candidates who meet the age and educational criteria should read the complete notification and apply online at the official website.

Who should read this

  • Graduates aged 20-30 years aspiring for a banking career as Probationary Officers

How to apply

  1. Visit the official website www.ibps.in
  2. Click on the Home page to open the link 'CRP PO/MT'
  3. Click on 'CLICK HERE TO APPLY ONLINE FOR CRP-Probationary Officers/Management Trainees (CRP-PO/MT-XVI)' to open the On-Line Application Form
  4. Choose 'CLICK HERE FOR NEW REGISTRATION' and enter basic details
  5. Upload photograph, signature, left thumb impression and a hand-written declaration
  6. Proceed to pay the application fee online
  7. Submit and take a printout of the final application form

Documents to keep ready

  • Photograph
  • Signature
  • Left thumb impression
  • Hand-written declaration
  • Photo ID proof
  • Educational certificates

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not wait till the last date to apply online.
  • Ensure category and details filled match original documents as changes are not allowed after the edit window closes.

Good to know

What is IBPS CRP PO/MT?

IBPS CRP PO/MT is a Common Recruitment Process conducted annually by the Institute of Banking Personnel Selection to select Probationary Officers and Management Trainees for participating public sector banks.

What happens next

  1. Qualify Preliminary Examination to appear for Main Examination
  2. Qualify Main Examination to attend Personality Test and Interview
  3. Provisional Allotment based on merit and vacancies

जानने योग्य

आईबीपीएस सीआरपी पीओ/एमटी क्या है?

आईबीपीएस सीआरपी पीओ/एमटी सहभागी सार्वजनिक क्षेत्र के बैंकों के लिए परिवीक्षाधीन अधिकारियों और प्रबंधन प्रशिक्षुओं का चयन करने के वास्ते इंस्टीट्यूट ऑफ बैंकर्स पर्सनल सिलेक्शन द्वारा प्रतिवर्ष आयोजित एक सामान्य भर्ती प्रक्रिया है।

आगे क्या होगा

  1. मुख्य परीक्षा में बैठने के लिए प्रारंभिक परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण करें
  2. पर्सनलिटी टेस्ट और साक्षात्कार में भाग लेने के लिए मुख्य परीक्षा उत्तीर्ण करें
  3. मेरिट और रिक्तियों के आधार पर अस्थायी आवंटन
