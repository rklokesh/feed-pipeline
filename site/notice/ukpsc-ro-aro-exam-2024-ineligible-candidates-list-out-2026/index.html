About this notice

The Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC) has released the list of ineligible and rejected candidates following the document verification for the Review Officer / Assistant Review Officer Examination 2024 held for the Advocate General Office, Nainital. Candidates who wish to submit representations along with supporting documents can do so by post or email until 13 November 2026. No representations will be accepted after the deadline.

Who should read this

  • Candidates who appeared for the UKPSC RO/ARO Exam 2024 Document Verification and want to check their status

Who cannot apply

  • Candidates submitting representations after 13 November 2026, 06:00 PM

How to download

  1. Check the ineligible/rejected candidates list on the official UKPSC website.
  2. Prepare your representation along with supporting documents and certificates.
  3. Submit the documents by post to the office of Secretary, Uttarakhand Public Service Commission, Haridwar, Pin Code - 249404, OR send via email to soexamtwo2@gmail.com by 13 Nov 2026, 06:00 PM.

Documents to keep ready

  • Representation application
  • Supporting certificates and documents for claim verification

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not submit representations after the deadline of 13 November 2026 (06:00 PM).
  • Ensure supporting documents are attached with the representation.
