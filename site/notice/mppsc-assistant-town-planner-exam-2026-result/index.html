About this notice

The Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC) has announced the written exam result for the Assistant Town Planner Exam 2026 held on 20 September 2026. A total of 39 vacancies are being filled through this recruitment. Candidates provisionally shortlisted for the interview must send their hard copy application forms along with required documents to the Commission office by 17 November 2026.

How to download
  1. Check your roll number in the official result PDF.
  2. Download and print the application form and checklist from the MPPSC website.
  3. Attach self-attested copies of all required certificates and mark sheets.
  4. Send the complete application packet by speed post or submit in person to the Commission office by 17 Nov 2026.
Documents to keep ready
  • Application form printout
  • Educational qualification certificates
  • Age proof
  • Caste/Category certificate if applicable
  • Other supporting documents as per instructions
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Failing to submit the hard copy of application and documents by 17 Nov 2026
  • Forgetting to attach self-attested copies of certificates
