About this notice

Central Industrial Security Force (CISF) has released the recruitment notification for Paramedical Staff posts for the year 2026. A total of 24 temporary yet pensionable vacancies for Assistant Sub Inspector posts in Pharmacist, X-Ray Technician, and Lab Technician trades are to be filled. Online applications are open from 08 June 2026 and will close on 07 July 2026. Eligible male and Indian citizen candidates meeting the required educational qualifications and physical standards can apply online through the CISF recruitment website.

Who should read this

  • Eligible Indian citizens holding required qualifications in Pharmacy, Radiography or Medical Laboratory Technology aged 18-28 years.

Who cannot apply

  • Candidates who do not possess the required educational qualifications or age criteria as on the cut-off date.

Important dates

Online application starts08 Jun 2026
Last date to apply07 Jul 2026 (23:59 hrs)

Age limit

Age limit for ASI (Pharmacist, X-Ray Technician, Lab Technician)18 to 28 years as on 07 Jul 2026

Assistant Sub Inspector (Pharmacist)18 · UR 10, EWS 01, OBC 04, SC 02, ST 01, Ex-Servicemen 02
Assistant Sub Inspector (X-Ray Technician)01 · UR 01, EWS 00, OBC 00, SC 00, ST 00, Ex-Servicemen 00
Assistant Sub Inspector (Lab Technician)05 · UR 04, EWS 00, OBC 01, SC 00, ST 00, Ex-Servicemen 00

Eligibility

ASI (Pharmacist)Passed 12th class with Diploma or Degree in Pharmacy from a recognized institution and registered as a Pharmacist under the Pharmacy Act, 1948.
ASI (X-Ray Technician)Passed 12th class with 2 years Diploma in Radiography from a recognized institution.
ASI (Lab Technician)Passed 12th class with 2 years Diploma in Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT) from a recognized institution.

Physical standards

Height (Male - UR, EWS, SC, OBC)170 cm
Chest (Male - UR, EWS, SC, OBC)80 - 85 cm
Height (Female - UR, EWS, SC, OBC)157 cm

Salary and pay level

Pay ScaleLevel-5 (₹29,200 – 92,300)

Selection process

  1. CBT / OMR Based Written Examination
  2. Physical Standards Test (PST)
  3. Document Verification
  4. Trade Test (BOHV)
  5. Detailed Medical Examination (DME) / Review Medical Examination (RME)

How to download

  1. Visit the official CISF recruitment website at https://cisfrectt.cisf.gov.in.
  2. Register and fill out the online application form with required details.
  3. Upload necessary scanned documents and photographs as per instructions.
  4. Submit the application form before the last date and take a printout for future reference.

Documents to keep ready

  • Educational certificates and marksheets
  • Diploma or degree certificates
  • Pharmacy registration certificate
  • Identity proof
  • Category certificate (if applicable)

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Submitting applications through any mode other than online
  • Failing to check eligibility criteria before applying
  • Providing incorrect personal or educational details

Good to know

What is CISF Paramedical Staff Recruitment?

It is a direct recruitment drive conducted by the Central Industrial Security Force to select qualified candidates for Assistant Sub Inspector posts in trades like Pharmacist, X-Ray Technician, and Lab Technician.

What happens next

  1. Shortlisted candidates will be called for subsequent stages including physical tests, document verification, trade test, and medical examination.
