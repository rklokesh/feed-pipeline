About this notice

Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPBPB) has published the notice regarding the final marks for Homeguard Enrolment 2025. The final selection result was earlier declared on 03 Oct 2026 for a total of 41,424 vacancies. Candidates who participated in the enrolment process can view their final marks by logging in with their registration number and date of birth on the official website between 24 Oct 2026 and 23 Nov 2026. Applicants should keep their registered email active to receive the score details.
