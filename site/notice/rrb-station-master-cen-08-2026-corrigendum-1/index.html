About this notice

Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB) have published Corrigendum No. 1 for CEN No. 08/2026, which was originally published on 26.10.2026 for the recruitment of Station Master. Through this notice, the total number of vacancies has been revised and increased from 1,215 to 1,265 for all RRBs combined. Candidates can check the updated vacancy position in the official corrigendum. All other terms and conditions of the notification remain unchanged.

Good to know

What is RRB CEN 08/2026 Corrigendum No. 1?

It is an official notice issued by Railway Recruitment Boards to correct an inadvertent error in the total vacancy count for the Station Master recruitment, raising it from 1,215 to 1,265.

What is the age limit for RRB Station Master CEN 08/2026?

The prescribed age limit is 20 to 33 years as on 01.01.2027.
