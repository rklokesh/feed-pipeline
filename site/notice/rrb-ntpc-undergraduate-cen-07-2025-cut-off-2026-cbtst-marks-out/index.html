About this notice

Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Ahmedabad has officially released the cut-off marks for candidates shortlisted for the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBTST) under Centralised Employment Notice CEN 07/2025 for NTPC Under Graduate posts. The notice was issued on 30 October 2026. Candidates who appeared or awaited the shortlisting can check the category-wise cut-off marks out of 100 directly through the official RRB Ahmedabad website.

Who should read this

  • Candidates who appeared for RRB CEN 07/2025 NTPC Under Graduate exams and are awaiting CBTST shortlisting.

Important dates

Notice Date30 Oct 2026

Selection process

  1. Computer Based Test (CBT)
  2. Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBTST)

How to download

  1. Visit the official website of RRB Ahmedabad.
  2. Click on the link for CEN 07/2025 NTPC Under Graduate CBTST cut off marks.
  3. Download and check the category-wise cut off list.

Good to know

What is RRB NTPC Undergraduate CBTST Cut Off?

It is the minimum mark out of 100 required by candidates in the CBT stage to qualify and get shortlisted for the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBTST) under CEN 07/2025.

What happens next

  1. Shortlisted candidates will appear for the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBTST).
