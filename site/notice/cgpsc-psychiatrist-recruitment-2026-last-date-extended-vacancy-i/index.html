About this notice

Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission (CGPSC) has issued a corrigendum for the Psychiatrist recruitment under the Health and Family Welfare Department. The total number of vacancies has been increased from 6 to 8. Along with the vacancy increase, the online application dates have been revised. Candidates can now apply online from August 26, 2026, to September 15, 2026. Those who have already applied earlier do not need to apply again.

क्या जो उम्मीदवार पहले आवेदन कर चुके हैं, उन्हें दोबारा आवेदन करने की आवश्यकता है?
