About this notice

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has published the reserve list and cut-off marks for the Lecturer (Prasooti Tantra and Stree Roga) Competitive Examination 2025 under the Ayurved, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy (AYUSH) Department. Document verification was conducted for candidates from the consideration list, following which the reserve list was prepared. One candidate has been declared successful with a specified merit rank. The cut-off marks for the General (UR) category have also been released.
