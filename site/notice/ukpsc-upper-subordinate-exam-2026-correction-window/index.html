UKPSC Upper Subordinate Exam 2026 Correction Window

Correction window open till 16 Oct 2026

Uttarakhand Public Service Commission has opened the online correction window for the Combined State Civil Upper Subordinate Examination 2026. Candidates who applied by September 29, 2026, can edit their applications from October 7 to October 16, 2026.

About this notice

Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC) has issued a detailed notification regarding the correction window for the Combined State Civil Upper Subordinate Examination 2026. Candidates who successfully submitted their online applications by the last date of September 29, 2026, can now edit their details. The online edit window is available on the commission website from October 7, 2026, to October 16, 2026.

How to apply
  1. Visit the official UKPSC website at psc.uk.gov.in.
  2. Log in using your registered Email ID and Password.
  3. Make the necessary corrections in your application data (except mobile number and email ID).
  4. Pay additional fee if category/subcategory change requires it, and click Final Submit.

Frequently asked questions
What are the dates for the UKPSC Upper Subordinate 2026 correction window?

The correction window is open from October 7, 2026, to October 16, 2026.

Who is eligible to use the edit window?
