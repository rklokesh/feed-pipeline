About this notice

Railway Recruitment Cell, Western Railway (RRC WR) has released an official notice regarding the rescheduling of the Computer Based Test (CBT) for the post of Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP) and Technician-III under GDCE Notification No. 01/2023. As per the notice, the exam previously scheduled for 07-11-2026 has been postponed and will now be conducted on 13-11-2026. Candidates are advised to check the official website for call letter updates.

What changed
CBT Exam Date07 Nov 2026 → 13 Nov 2026
Who should read this
  • Registered departmental candidates of Western Railway for ALP and Tech-III posts
Who cannot apply
  • Employees who have been transferred from Western Railway to Other Zonal Railways / PUs are not eligible to appear in the CBT.

How to download
  1. Visit the official RRC Western Railway website at www.rrc-wr.com.
  2. Click on the call letter download link available 7 days before the exam.
  3. Log in using your credentials to download and print your admit card.
Documents to keep ready
  • Printed copy of the CBT call letter
  • Valid Photo ID proof
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Do not wait until the last day to download the admit card.
  • Ensure you are not transferred to another zone before appearing, as candidature will be rejected at the DV stage.
