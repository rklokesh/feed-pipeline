Western Railway ALP and Tech-III CBT Rescheduling Notice 2026

Updated 23 Oct 2026 · Source: rrc-wr.com

CBT exam rescheduled to 13 Nov 2026

Western Railway has rescheduled the computer-based test for ALP and Tech-III posts under GDCE Notification No. 01/2023. The exam originally scheduled for 07-11-2026 will now be held on 13-11-2026. Eligible candidates can download their call letters from the official website 7 days before the exam.

About this notice

Railway Recruitment Cell, Western Railway (RRC WR) has released a notice regarding the rescheduling of the Computer Based Test (CBT) for the post of ALP and Tech-III under GDCE Notification No. 01/2023. As per the notice, the exam previously set for 07-11-2026 is now postponed to 13-11-2026. Candidates should check the official website for call letter downloads and further updates.

How to download
  1. Visit the official website of RRC Western Railway at www.rrc-wr.com.
  2. Click on the link to download the call letter, which will be available 7 days before the exam date.
  3. Log in using your credentials and download your admit card for the CBT exam.

Frequently asked questions

When is the rescheduled CBT for ALP and Tech-III posts?

The CBT is rescheduled to be held on 13 November 2026.

When can I download my call letter for the RRC WR CBT exam?

The call letter download link will be available on the official website 7 days before the date of the CBT exam.

Who is not eligible to appear in the CBT exam?

Employees who have been transferred from Western Railway to other Zonal Railways or production units are not eligible to appear in the CBT.
