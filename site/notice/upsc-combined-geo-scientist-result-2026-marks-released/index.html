About this notice

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the marks of recommended candidates for the Combined Geo-Scientist Examination 2026. Candidates who appeared for the exam can check their written total, personality test marks, and final total scores. The list includes details of candidates recommended for various services including Geologist, Hydrogeologist, Geophysicist, Chemist, and Assistant Chemist.

Good to know

What is the UPSC Combined Geo-Scientist Examination?

It is a national-level competitive examination conducted by the UPSC for recruitment to various Group A and Group B posts in geological and related departments of the Government of India.
