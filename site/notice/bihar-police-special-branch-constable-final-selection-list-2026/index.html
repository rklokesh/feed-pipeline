About this notice

Central Selection Board of Constable (CSBC), Bihar has announced the final selection list for the recruitment of Constables (General Duty Close Cadre) in the Special Branch of Bihar Police under Advertisement No. 01/2026. The written examination was conducted on 24 June 2026, followed by the Physical Standard Test (PST) on 12 September 2026. A total of 83 candidates have been successfully recommended for final appointment. Selected candidates must report for joining with required documents between 25 October 2026 and 20 November 2026.

  • Candidates who appeared for the Bihar Police Special Branch Constable Advt. No. 01/2026 recruitment process.

How to download
  1. Visit the official CSBC Bihar website at https://csbc.bihar.gov.in.
  2. Check the Special Branch tab and click on the final selection list PDF notice for Advt. No. 01/2026.
  3. Search your roll number in the published list of selected candidates.
  4. Report for joining physically at the Special Branch with required original documents between 25 Oct 2026 and 20 Nov 2026.
Documents to keep ready
  • Educational qualification certificates
  • Date of birth proof
  • Caste and residential certificate (for SC/ST candidates)
  • Caste-cum-residential-cum-creamy layer free certificate (for BC and EBC candidates)
  • Economically Weaker Section (EWS) certificate
  • Government servant certificate (if applicable)
  • Freedom Fighter dependent certificate (if applicable)
  • Valid photo ID card (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, Driving Licence, etc.)
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Failing to report for joining within the stipulated window from 25 October 2026 to 20 November 2026.
  • Missing mandatory original certificates during physical verification at the joining venue.
