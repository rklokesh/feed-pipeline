About this notice

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has released the notification for the National Institute of Fashion Technology Entrance Examination (NIFTEE) 2027 on behalf of NIFT. Online applications are invited for admissions to various Undergraduate (UG), Postgraduate (PG), NIFT Lateral Entry Admissions (NLEA), Artisans category, and Ph.D. programmes for the academic session 2027-28. The online registration process starts on 16th October 2026 and will conclude on 16th November 2026. Candidates aspiring to join NIFT must review the detailed eligibility criteria, age limits, and guidelines in the official information bulletin before submitting their applications.

Who should read this
  • Students seeking admission to UG, PG, NLEA, Artisans, and Ph.D. programmes at NIFT for the academic session 2027-28.
Who cannot apply
  • Candidates who fail to meet the educational qualifications and age criteria specified in the NIFT Admissions Guidelines-2027.

Eligibility
General EligibilityRefer to the NIFT Admissions Guidelines–2027 and NIFTEE–2027 Information Bulletin for specific educational qualifications, age limits, and subject combinations.
Selection process
  1. Stage 1 Entrance Examination (Computer Based Test / Pen-and-Paper Based Test)
  2. Subsequent selection stages as prescribed in NIFT Admissions Guidelines-2027 (including Research Proposal Presentation and Interview for Ph.D.)
How to apply
  1. Go to the official NTA NIFTEE website at https://exams.nta.nic.in/niftee/
  2. Register using a valid email ID and mobile number to generate login credentials.
  3. Fill out the online application form and upload scanned documents including photograph, signature, and UDID card (if applicable).
  4. Complete Aadhaar-based e-KYC if required and submit the application fee.
  5. Download and print a copy of the confirmation page for future reference.
Documents to keep ready
  • Recent photograph
  • Signature
  • Aadhaar-based e-KYC details
  • UDID Card (for PwD/PwBD candidates)
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Submitting applications after the last date without factoring in the late fee window.
  • Uploading blurred or improperly formatted photographs and signatures.
  • Failing to check specific eligibility and subject combination requirements for chosen programmes.
Good to know

What is NIFTEE 2027?

The National Institute of Fashion Technology Entrance Examination (NIFTEE) is conducted by the National Testing Agency for admissions to various design, management, and technology programmes offered by NIFT institutes across India.

What happens next
  1. Candidates will appear for the entrance examination on 10 January 2027.
  2. NTA will release the recorded responses and answer keys, followed by the declaration of Stage 1 results on official websites.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to apply for NIFTEE 2027?

The last date to submit the online application without late fee is 16th November 2026.

When will the NIFTEE 2027 exam be conducted?

The entrance examination is scheduled to be held on 10th January 2027.

Who conducts the NIFTEE 2027 examination?

The examination is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) on behalf of NIFT.

Can I apply with a late fee?

Yes, applications can be submitted with a late fee of ₹5000/- up to 21st November 2026.

Admit cards are scheduled to be downloaded starting from 4th January 2027.

What are the official websites for NIFTEE 2027?

The official websites are https://www.nta.ac.in and https://exams.nta.nic.in/niftee/
