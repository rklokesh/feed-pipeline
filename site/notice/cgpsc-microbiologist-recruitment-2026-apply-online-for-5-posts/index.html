About this notice

Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission (CGPSC) has released advertisement number 05/2026 for the direct recruitment of Microbiologists under the Health and Family Welfare Department for State Food and Drug Testing Laboratories. There are a total of 5 vacancies available for Food Laboratory and Drug Laboratory. Eligible and interested candidates can submit their online applications through the official website www.psc.cg.gov.in from 10 September 2026 to 9 October 2026. Applicants should ensure they meet all eligibility criteria before applying.

Who should read this

  • Candidates holding M.Sc. in Microbiology with 5 years of relevant laboratory testing experience

Who cannot apply

  • Candidates without the required educational qualifications and experience as of the last date of application

शैक्षणिक योग्यता (औषधि प्रयोगशाला)ड्रग्स एवं कॉस्मेटिक्स रूल्स, 1945 के उपबंधों के अधीन शासकीय विश्लेषक के रूप में नियुक्ति के लिए विहित अर्हता तथा पांच वर्ष का अनुभव, अथवा एम.एस.सी. (माइक्रो बायोलॉजी) तथा मान्यता प्राप्त प्रयोगशाला में औषधि परीक्षण का पांच वर्ष का अनुभव।
