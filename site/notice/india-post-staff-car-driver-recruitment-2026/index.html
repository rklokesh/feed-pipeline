About this notice

India Post, Office of the Manager, Mail Motor Service, Bengaluru has released a notification for filling up 11 vacancies of Staff Car Driver (Ordinary Grade) on a deputation/absorption basis. The posts belong to General Central Services, Group 'C', Non-Gazetted, Non-Ministerial in Pay Level-2. Eligible and willing regular central government employees can submit their applications in the prescribed proforma through proper channel within 60 days of publication in Employment News. Applicants should ensure they meet all eligibility conditions including a valid driving license and required experience.

Who should read this
  • Regular Dispatch Riders (Group 'C') and MTS (Group 'C') in Pay Level-1 of Department of Posts or other Central Government Ministries and Armed Forces personnel.
Who cannot apply
  • Candidates who apply without proper channel or without required certificates and documents will not be considered.

How to apply
  1. Check eligibility and download the application proforma from www.indiapost.gov.in.
  2. Fill out the application in the prescribed proforma (Annexure-I) in duplicate.
  3. Attach required documents certified by a Gazetted Officer including educational certificate, driving license, and experience certificate.
  4. Send the application through proper channel via Speed Post only to the Manager, Mail Motor Service, No. 4, Basaveshwara Road, Vasanthnagar, Bengaluru - 560001 within 60 days.
Documents to keep ready
  • Integrity certificate
  • List of major/minor penalties imposed during last 10 years (or 'Nil' certificate)
  • Vigilance clearance certificate
  • Attested copies of APAR for last 5 years (2020-21 to 2024-25)
  • Experience certificate for 3 years certified by Gazetted Officer
  • Valid LMV and HMV driving license certified by Gazetted Officer
  • 10th standard certificate certified by Gazetted Officer
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Do not send applications by ordinary post or courier; use Speed Post only.
  • Ensure applications are sent through proper channel with all required enclosures attested by a Gazetted Officer.

Frequently asked questions

How many vacancies are announced for India Post Staff Car Driver Recruitment 2026?

A total of 11 vacancies are announced (10 for MMS Bengaluru and 1 for MMS Sub Depot Mysuru).

What is the last date to apply?

Applications must be sent within 60 days from the date of publication of the advertisement in Employment News.

What is the age limit for this recruitment?

The maximum age limit for appointment by deputation/absorption shall not exceed 56 years as on the closing date of receipt of applications.

What is the educational qualification required?

Candidates must have passed 10th standard from a recognized board or institute, along with a valid driving license for light and heavy motor vehicles.

What is the pay scale for Staff Car Driver posts?

Selected candidates will get a salary in Pay Matrix Level-2 (₹19,900–63,200) as per the 7th CPC.

How should applications be sent?

Applications must be sent through proper channel via Speed Post only to the Manager, Mail Motor Service, Bengaluru.

What is the selection process?

Selection is based on a driving test and trade test as per prescribed pattern/syllabus.

Where can the application proforma be downloaded?

The application proforma and notification can be downloaded from the official website www.indiapost.gov.in.

Final checklist
  • Verify eligibility criteria including 10th pass, driving license, and experience.
  • Fill out Annexure-I completely and get Annexure-II/certificates attested.
  • Dispatch through Speed Post well before the 60-day deadline.
