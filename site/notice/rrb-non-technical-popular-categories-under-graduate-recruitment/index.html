About this notice

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has released Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 07/2026 for the recruitment of Non-Technical Popular Categories (Under Graduate) posts across various zones. A total of 1,688 vacancies are available for positions including Commercial Cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Junior Clerk Cum Typist, and Trains Clerk. Online applications begin on 15 October 2026 and will close on 13 November 2026. Under-graduate candidates aged between 18 and 30 years should read the official notification carefully before applying through the unified official RRB website.

Who should read this
  • Undergraduate candidates aged 18-30 years who want a railway job.
Who cannot apply
  • Candidates waiting for final results of prescribed educational qualifications are NOT eligible to apply.

How to apply
  1. Visit the unified official RRB website.
  2. Click on 'Create an Account' if you are a new user, or log in with existing credentials.
  3. Read the detailed CEN thoroughly before filling up the online application.
  4. Select your chosen RRB, Railway Zone/PU, and post preferences.
  5. Upload mandatory scanned documents, live photo, and pay the application fee.
  6. Submit the application and take a printout of the final confirmation.
Documents to keep ready
  • Educational qualification certificates
  • Age proof certificate
  • Caste / Category certificate in prescribed format
  • Scanned photograph and signature
  • Aadhaar or Digilocker identity details
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Submitting multiple applications to different RRBs or the same RRB will lead to rejection of all applications.
  • Details filled in the 'Create an Account' form and 'Chosen RRB' cannot be modified later.
  • Waiting for final results makes the candidate ineligible.
Good to know

What is RRB NTPC Under Graduate Recruitment?

It is a centralised recruitment process conducted by the Railway Recruitment Board to select eligible undergraduate candidates for various non-technical popular categories posts in Indian Railways.

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to apply for RRB NTPC Under Graduate Recruitment 2026?

The last date to submit online applications is 13 November 2026.

How many total vacancies are announced under CEN 07/2026?

A total of 1,688 vacancies are announced across all RRBs.

What is the age limit for RRB NTPC Under Graduate posts?

Candidates must be between 18 and 30 years of age as on 01 January 2027.

What is the educational qualification required?

Candidates must possess an Under Graduate qualification from a recognised board or university.

When does the online application process start?

The online application process starts on 15 October 2026.

Can I apply to multiple RRBs?

No, multiple applications to different RRBs or the same RRB will lead to rejection of all applications.
