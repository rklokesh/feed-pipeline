About this notice

The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPBPB) has released a notice regarding the Document Verification, Physical Standard Test (DV/PST), and Physical Efficiency Test (PET) for the Direct Recruitment 2020-21 of Sub Inspector Civil Police, PAC Platoon Commander, and Fire Officer II. This is in compliance with the Hon'ble High Court, Allahabad orders in Special Appeal No. 651/2026. The DV/PST will be held on 02 November 2026 at Reserve Police Lines, Lucknow, and the PET will be held on 05 November 2026 at 35th Battalion PAC, Lucknow. Eligible candidates must check their registered email for admit cards.

Who should read this
  • Candidates covered under High Court Special Appeal No. 651/2026 and Writ Petition No. 11427/2022 for UP Police SI 2020-21 recruitment.
Important dates
DV / PST Date02 Nov 2026
PET Date05 Nov 2026
Selection process
  1. Document Verification and Physical Standard Test (DV/PST)
  2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
How to download
  1. Check your registered e-mail ID for the admit card.
  2. Download and print the admit card along with instructions.
  3. Report at Reserve Police Lines, Lucknow on 02 Nov 2026 at 08:00 AM for DV/PST with original documents, certified copies, and photographs.
  4. Report at 35th Battalion PAC, Lucknow on 05 Nov 2026 at 06:00 AM for PET if qualified.
Documents to keep ready
  • Admit Card with instructions
  • Original documents
  • Certified photocopy of documents
  • Photographs
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Do not miss the given date, time, and venue as no alternative opportunity will be provided.

Frequently asked questions

When is the DV/PST for UP Police SI 2020-21 scheduled?

The DV/PST is scheduled for 02 November 2026 at Reserve Police Lines, Lucknow.

When is the PET scheduled?

The Physical Efficiency Test (PET) is scheduled for 05 November 2026 at 35th Battalion PAC, Lucknow.

How can I download my admit card?

Admit cards are being sent directly to the candidates' registered e-mail IDs.
