About this notice

The Haryana Staff Selection Commission (HSSC) has published the final answer key for the Primary Teacher (PRT) recruitment examination held under advertisement number 5/2024. Candidates who appeared for the exam can now verify their responses with the officially released answers for sets A, B, C, and D. This final answer key serves as the basis for the evaluation of the exam papers. All applicants should check the correct answers provided for all 100 questions across the different sets.

Who should read this
  • Candidates who appeared for the HSSC PRT exam under Advt 5/2024
Important dates
Notice Date10 Sep 2026
How to download
  1. Review the official answer key PDF for your respective question paper set (Set A, B, C, or D).
  2. Match your marked answers with the correct options given in the notice.
Good to know

What is the HSSC PRT Final Answer Key?

It is the official list of correct answers released by the Haryana Staff Selection Commission for the Primary Teacher exam under advertisement 5/2024 after reviewing objections.
