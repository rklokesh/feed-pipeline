About this notice

The Rajasthan Staff Selection Board (RSSB) has released a notification regarding the online scrutiny form for the Driver Recruitment 2024. A total of 4,508 candidates have been shortlisted based on merit following the trade test for 2,756 posts. Shortlisted candidates must fill out the detailed online scrutiny form through their SSO ID on the Recruitment Portal between October 30, 2026, and November 12, 2026. Candidates are also required to submit necessary documents, affidavits, and the prescribed experience declaration form during document verification.

Selection process

How to download

  1. Log in to your SSO ID on the Rajasthan Recruitment Portal.
  2. Fill out the online scrutiny form (सहपरिनिरिक्षा फॉर्म) between 30 Oct 2026 and 12 Nov 2026.
  3. Keep the required affidavits, experience declaration form, and original documents ready for document verification.

Documents to keep ready

  • Online Scrutiny Form printout
  • Driving Licence
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Experience declaration form (Annexure-1) issued by State Motor Garage Department or valid employer
  • Notarized affidavits for unmarried or married status on ₹50 non-judicial stamp paper
  • Educational and category certificates

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not miss the online scrutiny form deadline of 12 November 2026.
  • Ensure affidavits are printed on ₹50 non-judicial stamp paper and notarized.

Good to know

What is the RSSB Driver Scrutiny Form Notice?

It is an official notification instructing shortlisted candidates of the Driver Recruitment 2024 to fill out their online scrutiny forms and prepare necessary documents and affidavits for document verification.

What happens next

  1. Separate schedule for document verification will be released by the board.
  2. Candidates must attend document verification with original documents and mandatory declarations.
