About this notice

Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has published Result Notice No. 208 for the Junior Draftsman (Electric) post under Post Code 23/25 in the New Delhi Municipal Council (NDMC). The online examination was conducted on 25 Jul 2026, with a total of 18 candidates appearing for the test. According to the notice, based on the performance in the online examination, no candidates have been found suitable in UR, EWS, OBC, and ST categories. Candidates can check their individual marks by logging into their accounts on the OARS module.
