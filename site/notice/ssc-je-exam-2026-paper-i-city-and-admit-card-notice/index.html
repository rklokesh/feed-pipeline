About this notice

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has released the examination city details for the Junior Engineer Examination, 2026 Paper-I. The computer-based examination is scheduled to be held from 02.11.2026 to 06.11.2026 for various disciplines, while the exam for JE (Telecommunication) will be held on 30.11.2026 and 01.12.2026. Candidates can view their exam city by logging into the official website using their credentials. The admission certificates will be made available for download 2 to 3 days prior to the respective exam date.

How to download
  1. Go to the official website of Staff Selection Commission (https://ssc.gov.in/).
  2. Click on the login module for exam city or admission certificate.
  3. Enter your registration credentials to log in.
  4. View your exam city details and download/print your admit card when available.
Documents to keep ready
  • Printed copy of Admission Certificate
  • Valid photo identity proof
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Do not wait until the exam day to check your exam city.
  • Ensure Scribe OTR mapping is completed by the deadline if availing the scribe facility.
Good to know

What is SSC JE Exam City Notice?

The exam city notice informs registered candidates about the city where their examination center is located so they can make travel arrangements in advance. Staff Selection Commission releases this slip a few days before the admit card.

What happens next
  1. Appear for the Computer Based Examination on the scheduled date and shift
  2. Wait for the release of answer keys and final results

Frequently asked questions

When is the SSC JE Paper-I Exam 2026 scheduled?

The SSC JE Paper-I Exam 2026 is scheduled to be held from 02.11.2026 to 06.11.2026.

How can I check my SSC JE exam city?

You can check your exam city by logging in through the designated login module on the official SSC website (https://ssc.gov.in/).

When will the SSC JE Admission Certificate be available?

The Admission Certificate will tentatively be available for download 2 to 3 days prior to the respective examination date.

What are the exam dates for JE (Telecommunication)?

The exam for the post of JE (Telecommunication) is scheduled on 30th November 2026 and 1st December 2026.

What is the last date to map Scribe OTR for candidates opting for own scribe?

The last date to map Scribe OTR is 27.10.2026 up to 11:00 PM.
