About this notice

The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released the main list and cut-off marks for the Lecturer (Kaumarbhritya) Competitive Examination 2025 under the Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy (AYUSH) Department. Candidates who appeared for document verification can check their roll number and merit rank in the result. The cut-off marks for the General (UR) category has been set at 87.00.
