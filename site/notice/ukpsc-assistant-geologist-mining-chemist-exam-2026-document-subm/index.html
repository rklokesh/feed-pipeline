About this notice

The Uttarakhand Public Service Commission (UKPSC) has issued a detailed notification regarding the document submission for the Assistant Geologist, Mining Officer, and Assistant Chemist (Group-B) Examination 2026 under the Directorate of Geology and Mining, Uttarakhand. Candidates who missed submitting their hard copies earlier due to postal disruptions during the Kanwar Yatra can now download their online application forms from the official website and send them along with required self-attested documents. The last date to submit the documents to the Commission office is 21 October 2026 by 06:00 PM. Those who have already submitted their documents earlier need not send them again.

How to apply

  4. Write the name of the exam, post name, and application number on top of the envelope

Frequently asked questions

What is the last date to submit documents for the UKPSC Assistant Geologist Exam 2026?

The last date to submit documents is 21 October 2026 up to 06:00 PM.

Who needs to submit the documents?

Candidates who applied for the Assistant Geologist, Mining Officer, and Assistant Chemist Exam 2026 and could not submit their documents earlier due to postal disruptions can submit them now.

Do candidates who already submitted documents need to resend them?

No, candidates who have already submitted their documents in response to the earlier notice dated 03 August 2026 do not need to resend them.

Where should the documents be sent?

The documents must be sent to the office of Secretary, Uttarakhand Public Service Commission, Haridwar-249404.

यूकेपीएससी सहायक भू-वैज्ञानिक परीक्षा 2026 के लिए दस्तावेज जमा करने की अंतिम तिथि क्या है?

दस्तावेज जमा करने की अंतिम तिथि 21 अक्टूबर 2026 सायं 06:00 बजे तक है।

किन्हें दस्तावेज जमा करने की आवश्यकता है?

सहायक भू-वैज्ञानिक, खान अधिकारी और सहायक रसायनज्ञ परीक्षा 2026 के वे अभ्यर्थी जो पूर्व में डाक सेवा बाधित होने के कारण दस्तावेज नहीं भेज पाए थे, वे अब भेज सकते हैं।

क्या जिन अभ्यर्थियों ने पहले ही दस्तावेज भेज दिए हैं, उन्हें दोबारा भेजने की आवश्यकता है?

नहीं, जिन अभ्यर्थियों ने 03 अगस्त 2026 की पूर्व विज्ञप्ति के क्रम में पहले ही अभिलेख भेज दिए हैं, उन्हें पुनः भेजने की आवश्यकता नहीं है।

दस्तावेज कहां भेजने हैं?

दस्तावेज सचिव, उत्तराखंड लोक सेवा आयोग, हरिद्वार-249404 के कार्यालय में भेजने हैं।
