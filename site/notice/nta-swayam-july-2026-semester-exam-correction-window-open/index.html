About this notice

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has announced a one-time correction facility for the registered candidates of the SWAYAM July 2026 Semester Examination. Candidates can modify specific details in their online application forms from 06 November to 07 November 2026 up to 11:50 P.M. The examination is scheduled to be conducted on 08, 09, 10, 11, and 12 December 2026 in CBT mode across various cities in India. Registered candidates are advised to visit the official website and verify their particulars to avoid any discrepancies.

Who should read this

  • Registered candidates who need to correct details in their SWAYAM July 2026 application form

Who cannot apply

  • Candidates are NOT allowed to change Mobile Number and E-Mail address

Important dates

Notice Date06 November 2026
Correction Window Starts06 November 2026
Correction Window Ends07 November 2026 (11:50 P.M.)
Exam Date08, 09, 10, 11 and 12 December 2026

Selection process

  1. CBT Mode Examination
  2. Result Declaration

How to download

  1. Visit the official SWAYAM NTA website at https://exams.nta.nic.in/swayam/
  2. Log in using your application number and password.
  3. Click on the correction link and make the required changes in the allowed fields.
  4. Pay any additional fee if applicable due to changes in category, PwD status, or courses.
  5. Submit the form and save a copy for future reference.

Documents to keep ready

  • Application Number and Password
  • Corrected details and supporting documents if required

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not attempt to change unchangeable fields like Mobile Number and Email Address.
  • Ensure all changes are verified before final submission as it is a one-time facility.

Good to know

What is the SWAYAM July 2026 Semester Exam Correction Window?

It is a limited period facility provided by NTA allowing registered candidates to rectify errors in specific particulars of their submitted online application forms.

What happens next

  1. Conduct of examination from 08 to 12 December 2026
  2. Release of answer key and subsequent result declaration

Frequently asked questions

When is the correction window open for SWAYAM July 2026 exam?

The correction window is open from 06 November to 07 November 2026 up to 11:50 P.M.

Can I change my mobile number and email address?

No, mobile numbers and email addresses cannot be changed.

What fields are allowed to be changed?

Candidates can change candidate name, father's name, mother's name, photograph, signature, date of birth, gender, category, sub-category/PwD status, university & college details, examination cities, and courses.

When will the SWAYAM July 2026 semester exam be conducted?

The exam will be conducted on 08, 09, 10, 11, and 12 December 2026.

Will I have to pay an additional fee for corrections?

An additional fee will be charged if changes in category, PwD status, or courses impact the fee amount.

Final checklist

  • Verify all editable fields carefully
  • Check for additional fee requirements if changing category or courses
  • Submit before 07 November 2026 (11:50 P.M.)

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

स्वाध्याय जुलाई 2026 परीक्षा के लिए करेक्शन विंडो कब खुली है?

करेक्शन विंडो 06 नवंबर से 07 नवंबर 2026 को रात्रि 11:50 बजे तक खुली है।

क्या मैं अपना मोबाइल नंबर और ईमेल पता बदल सकता हूँ?

नहीं, मोबाइल नंबर और ईमेल पता नहीं बदला जा सकता है।

किन फ़ील्ड को बदलने की अनुमति है?

उम्मीदवार उम्मीदवार का नाम, पिता का नाम, माता का नाम, फोटोग्राफ, हस्ताक्षर, जन्म तिथि, लिंग, श्रेणी, उप-श्रेणी/दिव्यांगता स्थिति, विश्वविद्यालय और कॉलेज विवरण, परीक्षा शहर और पाठ्यक्रम बदल सकते हैं।

स्वाध्याय जुलाई 2026 सेमेस्टर परीक्षा कब आयोजित की जाएगी?

परीक्षा 08, 09, 10, 11 और 12 दिसंबर 2026 को आयोजित की जाएगी।

क्या मुझे सुधारों के लिए अतिरिक्त शुल्क देना होगा?

श्रेणी, दिव्यांगता स्थिति या पाठ्यक्रमों में बदलाव से शुल्क राशि प्रभावित होने पर अतिरिक्त शुल्क लिया जाएगा।

  • सभी संपादन योग्य फ़ील्ड की सावधानीपूर्वक जांच करें
  • श्रेणी या पाठ्यक्रम बदलते समय अतिरिक्त शुल्क आवश्यकताओं की जांच करें
  • 07 नवंबर 2026 (रात्रि 11:50 बजे) से पहले सबमिट करें
