About this notice

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has released Notice No. 212 regarding the declaration of marks for the Tier-I examination and the shortlisting of candidates for the Physical Endurance Test (PET). This notice pertains to the recruitment for the post of Forest Guard under Post Code 41/25. Candidates who appeared for the exam can now check their scores and qualification status for the next stage.

Who should read this

  • Candidates who appeared for the DSSSB Forest Guard Tier-I exam under Post Code 41/25.

Important dates

Notice Date04 Nov 2026

Selection process

  1. Tier-I Examination
  2. Physical Endurance Test (PET)
  3. Document Verification and Medical Examination

How to download

  1. Visit the official website of DSSSB at dsssb.delhi.gov.in.
  2. Navigate to the 'Result' or 'Circulars' section.
  3. Click on Result Notice No. 212 for Post Code 41/25 Forest Guard.
  4. Download the PDF notice and check your roll number and marks.

Documents to keep ready

  • Admit card of the exam
  • Valid photo ID proof
  • Documents required for subsequent PET and verification stages

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Do not forget to check both your individual marks and your shortlisting status for the PET.

Good to know

What is DSSSB Forest Guard Notice No. 212?

Notice No. 212 is the official declaration of Tier-I examination marks and the list of candidates shortlisted for the Physical Endurance Test (PET) for the Forest Guard post under Post Code 41/25.

What happens next

  1. Shortlisted candidates must appear for the Physical Endurance Test (PET) as per the schedule announced by DSSSB.
