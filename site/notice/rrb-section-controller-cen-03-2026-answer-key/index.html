About this notice

Railway Recruitment Boards (RRB) has announced the availability of question papers, responses, and answer keys for the Computer Based Test (CBT) held under CEN 03/2026 for Section Controller posts in Level 6 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix. The exam was conducted from 12.10.2026 to 13.10.2026. Candidates who appeared for the examination can view their response sheets and raise online objections along with the prescribed fee from 26.10.2026 to 03.11.2026. Read on to know the complete schedule, fee details, and procedure for raising objections.

How to download
  1. Visit the official website of your respective Railway Recruitment Board (RRB).
  2. Click on the link for viewing question paper, responses, and answer keys / raising objections for CEN 03/2026.
  3. Log in using your credentials.
  4. View your response sheet and submit objections along with the prescribed fee before 03.11.2026 (05:00 PM).
Documents to keep ready
  • Candidate Login Credentials (Registration Number, Password/Date of Birth)
  • Online Payment Details for Fee Submission (Debit Card, Credit Card, UPI, Net Banking)
Common mistakes to avoid
  • Do not wait until the last moment to raise objections; complete the process well before 03.11.2026 at 05:00 PM.
  • Ensure objections are supported with valid details as the decision of RRBs shall be final.
Good to know

What is the RRB Section Controller CEN 03/2026 Objection Tracker?

It is an online portal provided by Railway Recruitment Boards allowing candidates who appeared for the Section Controller CBT to view their attempted answers, official answer keys, and submit objections against any discrepancies.

What happens next
  1. RRBs will review all valid objections received from candidates.
  2. Based on the reviewed objections, a final answer key will be prepared and the final result will be declared.
Other details

Frequently asked questions

When was the RRB Section Controller CEN 03/2026 CBT conducted?

The Computer Based Test was conducted from 12.10.2026 to 13.10.2026.

When can I view the question paper and raise objections?

The link is active from 26.10.2026 (05:00 PM) to 03.11.2026 (05:00 PM).

What is the fee for raising objections?

The prescribed fee is Rs. 50/- plus applicable bank service charges per question.

Will the objection fee be refunded?

Yes, if the objection raised is found to be correct, the fee paid will be refunded after deducting bank charges.

What is the last date to submit objections?

The last date to submit objections and pay the fee is 03.11.2026 up to 05:00 PM.

Can I raise objections after the closing date?

No, no representations will be entertained after 03.11.2026 at 05:00 PM.

What modes of payment are allowed for the objection fee?

Debit cards, credit cards, Rupay cards, UPI, and net banking (multi-bank and State Bank of India) are allowed.

Where can I find the objection tracker link?

The link is provided on the official websites of the respective Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs).

Final checklist
  • Check your responses against the official answer key.
  • Keep your online payment method ready if raising any objection.
  • Submit objections before 03.11.2026 (05:00 PM).
