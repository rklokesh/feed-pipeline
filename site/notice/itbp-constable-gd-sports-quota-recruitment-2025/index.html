ITBP Constable GD Sports Quota Recruitment 2025

Notice published

Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force invites online applications for 126 Constable General Duty posts under Sports Quota 2025. Eligible candidates can apply online from 3rd October 2026 to 1st November 2026.

About this notice

The Indo-Tibetan Border Police Force (ITBPF) has released a detailed advertisement for the recruitment of meritorious sportspersons to the post of Constable (General Duty) under Sports Quota-2025. There are 126 vacancies available across various sports disciplines. Online applications are open from 3rd October 2026 to 1st November 2026. Eligible and interested candidates should check their eligibility and apply online through the official ITBP recruitment website.
