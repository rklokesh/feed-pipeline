# Competitor review (28 Oct 2026)

Looked at: SarkariResult, FreeJobAlert, RojgarResult, SarkariExam (app listing only — site blocks fetching),
SarkariNaukri, Adda247, Testbook, Careers360, NCS, Jagran Josh (app listing), plus Play/App Store reviews.

## What they have that we don't (ranked by likely impact)

1. **Qualification pages** (10th, 12th, ITI, Diploma, Graduate) with live counts — FreeJobAlert's biggest hubs;
   readers ask for this filter. Needs a "minimum qualification" tag per notice (AI, during page writing).
2. **State pages** for the Hindi belt (UP, Bihar, Rajasthan, MP, Haryana, Delhi, Uttarakhand, HP, Punjab, J&K).
   Every competitor has them. Easy: board → state map.
3. **"What's next" per exam** ("Admit card: expected", "Result: released") — FreeJobAlert's status page answers
   "admit card kab aayega". Our exam pages can show the next stage, marked "expected" only when the notice gives a date.
4. **Hindi notice PDF + form-filling video link** in the links table (SarkariResult, RojgarResult).
5. **Alerts without an account** — web push / email filtered by state + qualification (FreeJobAlert needs an app).
6. **Cut-off archive per exam** (past years) — Careers360, Testbook, Adda. Harder: needs old PDFs.
7. **Tools**: age-eligibility checker (DOB vs cut-off date + category relaxation), photo/signature resizer (in-browser).
8. **Syllabus / exam-pattern pages per exam** — separate URLs on Careers360/Testbook/Adda.

## Their weaknesses (our edge — keep it)

- Ads everywhere ("whole screen gets covered by ads" — FreeJobAlert app reviews; SarkariResult app forces ads).
- Fake / clickbait alerts; PIB Fact Check warns about fake recruitment notices. We publish only from official PDFs.
- Stale or wrong data (SarkariResult app trending 2025 vacancies in mid-2026; Careers360 tables not updated; typos).
- Clutter: same listings repeated, menus shown 2–4 times; Testbook/Adda mostly sell courses.
- Little real Hindi: post bodies English-only. We are fully bilingual.
- Broken basics: blank app homepages, no search, dead links.

## SEO patterns worth copying

- Titles: `[Org] [Post] Recruitment 2026 Notification Out – Apply Online for [N] Posts`; `[Exam] Admit Card 2026 Out`.
  Status word ("Out") + vacancy count in the title.
- Hub URLs that rank: `/10th-pass-govt-jobs/`, `/up-govt-jobs/`, `/admit-card/`, `/answer-key/`, `/notification-status/`.
- One evolving page per recruitment (SarkariResult) vs one URL per stage (Careers360) — we do both: notice pages + exam hub.
- Trust lines: "Officially verified", updated time, quick summary box. We have these; add "checked against the official PDF".
- Monthly calendar posts ("Govt Jobs November 2026") — can be generated from our dates.
